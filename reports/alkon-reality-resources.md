# Alkon Reality Resources

Allowed resources:
- Codex / Local Builder when governed by Command Passport.
- Reports and evidence.
- Visual proof screenshots.
- Tests and route smoke.
- Approved local licensed assets with complete manifest metadata.

Forbidden resources:
- Payments / live trading as conversion resource.
- Web-triggered shell.
- Web-triggered Codex.
- Unknown-license assets.
- External hotlinks.
- Secrets or bank/card data.
- Fake claims.

Boundary:
Resources may help convert an idea into reality only when safe, lawful, useful, evidence-backed, and Product Truth preserving.

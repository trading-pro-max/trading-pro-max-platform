# Pro Max Trading Platform Depth Audit

Audit result: platform depth exists as paper-safe terminal depth, not live trading capability.

Keep:
- Market Board / Watchlist.
- Paper Order Ticket.
- Paper action buttons.
- Open Paper Positions and History.
- Activity/Audit dock.
- Assistant and Journal/Coach as secondary support.

Postpone:
- Real market depth from broker/feed.
- Live order routing.
- Account funding.
- Broker connection.
- Billing or plan activation.

Reason:
Depth must make Pro Max Trading feel like a real workspace without pretending live market execution exists.

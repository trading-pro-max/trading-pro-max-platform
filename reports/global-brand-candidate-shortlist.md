# Global Brand Candidate Shortlist

Status: empty_ready_for_intake

No final global brand candidate is adopted.

Candidate intake rules:

- invented or rare
- not generic
- not Pro Max
- not close to Apple, Dell, Alcon, or Alkon
- no implied regulation
- no fake Swiss legal claim
- not trading-only if it must become a world brand
- pronounceable in Arabic, English, and German
- domain-friendly
- trademark-friendly
- expandable into product lines

Ahmad approval is required before any candidate becomes trial or public brand.

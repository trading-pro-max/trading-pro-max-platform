# Al-Kawn Control Surfaces

## Why This Step Follows Visual Identity Unification

Ahmad asked for الكون to become controllable, not only visible. The unified visual identity made the surfaces feel like one system; Control Surfaces now give each core layer a structured private operating panel.

This mission does not start Infinity Mode, Operator Mode, native desktop finalization, mobile apps, Local Day One, billing, payments, receiving money, real money, broker execution, public launch, legal approval, FINMA approval, or brand adoption.

- `lib/server/universe/control-surfaces/*`

1. Root / Constitution Control Surface
2. Kernel Control Surface

7. Swiss Local Constitution Control Surface

Each surface includes state, owner layer, direct internal actions, legal stop actions, money stop actions, blocked actions, Product Truth impact, next safe action, and evidence route/report/test.

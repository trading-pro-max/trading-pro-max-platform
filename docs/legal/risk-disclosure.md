# Risk Disclosure

Trading and market analysis involve risk. Trading Pro Max currently supports paper-only evaluation and bounded decision support. It does not guarantee outcomes, profits, win rates, or risk reduction.

## Required Risk Truth

- Markets can move against any idea.
- Paper performance does not guarantee live performance.
- AI/IQ output is context support, not prediction certainty.
- Fallback data may be delayed, simulated, incomplete, or unsuitable for live trading.
- Live execution and real-money routing remain blocked unless explicitly configured and released in a future approved path.

## Disclosure Placement

Risk disclosure should appear where users may confuse support with execution or outcome:

- public entry
- execution ticket
- AI/IQ guidance
- journal and coach
- Pro/VIP roadmap
- media content
- academy lessons

## Blocked Wording

- safe profit
- guaranteed return
- no risk
- sure win
- protected from loss

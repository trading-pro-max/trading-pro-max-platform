# TPM Legal Counsel

TPM Legal Counsel is a product governance layer for safer wording, disclosure readiness, media review, and claim control. It is not a law firm, not legal advice, and not a replacement for qualified legal counsel.

## Legal Counsel Covers

- product claims
- media claims
- AI wording
- VIP wording
- Islamic account wording
- launch wording
- broker/feed wording
- billing/pricing wording
- community wording
- public communication

## Legal Counsel Blocks

- guaranteed profit
- win-rate claims
- risk-free language
- sure signal language
- fake live trading
- fake broker/feed/billing state
- fake public launch
- VIP guarantees results
- Islamic/Sharia certification without real certification
- AI predicts market with certainty
- legal advice
- financial advice

## Safe Language

- decision support
- context quality
- paper-only
- fallback-first
- not certified
- review required
- activation required
- operator review required
- not legal advice
- not financial advice

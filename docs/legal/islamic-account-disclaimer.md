# Islamic Account Disclaimer

Trading Pro Max may support account status labels for Standard, Islamic requested, Islamic review required, Islamic configured, Not certified, and Unavailable. These are product states, not religious or legal certification.

## Default Truth

- Standard account type may be shown for normal paper evaluation.
- Islamic/Sharia status is not certified by default.
- Islamic configured means configuration state only.
- Review required means qualified review is still needed.

## Blocked Claims

- Sharia certified
- Islamic compliant
- halal trading account
- approved by scholars
- certified swap-free product

These claims require real review, legal documentation, and qualified certification before use.

# Media Claims Policy

Media content must pass brand, Guardian, Legal, and Founder approval according to risk.

## Content Lifecycle

1. idea
2. draft
3. brand review
4. Guardian review
5. Legal review
6. Founder approval
7. scheduled
8. published
9. blocked
10. archived

## Risk Classification

- safe_auto_publish later: education, platform tips, paper-mode explanation, fallback/blocked explanation, academy content, non-sensitive updates.
- approval_required: Pro/VIP claims, AI capability claims, Islamic account wording, launch wording, broker/feed/live wording, billing/pricing, ads, performance discussion.
- blocked: guaranteed profit, win-rate, risk-free wording, sure signal, fake live trading, fake broker/feed/billing, fake public launch, fake Islamic/Sharia certification, misleading urgency, gambling-style marketing, financial advice, legal advice, copied competitor content.

## No External Publishing

This architecture does not connect social accounts, add tokens, schedule real posts, or publish externally.

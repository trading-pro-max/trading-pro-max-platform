# Media Claims Policy

Media content must pass brand, Guardian, Legal, and Founder approval according to risk.

## Content Lifecycle

1. idea
2. draft
3. brand review
4. Guardian review
5. Legal review
6. Founder approval
7. scheduled
8. published
9. blocked
10. archived

## Risk Classification

- safe_auto_publish later: education, platform tips, paper-mode explanation, fallback/blocked explanation, academy content, non-sensitive updates.
- approval_required: Pro/VIP claims, AI capability claims, Islamic account wording, launch wording, broker/feed/live wording, billing/pricing, ads, performance discussion.
- blocked: guaranteed profit, win-rate, risk-free wording, sure signal, fake live trading, fake broker/feed/billing, fake public launch, fake Islamic/Sharia certification, misleading urgency, gambling-style marketing, financial advice, legal advice, copied competitor content.

## No External Publishing

This architecture does not connect social accounts, add tokens, schedule real posts, or publish externally.

## Planet Earth Claim Boundary

Media Office, AI Video Studio, Community, Treasury, and Founder Command must use this same claims policy. No continent, state, ministry, profession, or assistant may publish or approve wording that implies fake launch, live trading, billing, broker/feed activation, performance guarantees, or certification.

## Runtime Rule Connection

Content Factory classifications must pass through Guardian, Legal, and Founder approval when claims touch Pro/VIP, AI capability, Islamic wording, launch, broker/feed/live, billing/pricing, ads, or performance discussion. External publishing remains blocked until real account connections and approvals exist.

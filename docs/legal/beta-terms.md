# Closed Beta Terms Readiness

These notes prepare future closed beta terms. They are not a public launch claim.

## Beta Truth

- beta access is limited
- paper-only execution remains default
- real money is blocked
- broker/feed/billing/public launch are inactive unless explicitly configured later
- feedback is account-scoped and reviewed
- users should not rely on beta behavior for live trading

## Required Beta Terms Topics

- eligibility and allowlist
- acceptable use
- no financial advice
- no guaranteed uptime
- feedback ownership and use
- privacy and data handling
- support expectations
- termination of beta access
- no public launch claim

## Founder Approval

Public beta terms require Founder and Legal review before use.

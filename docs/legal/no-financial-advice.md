# No Financial Advice

Trading Pro Max does not provide financial advice. Platform content, AI/IQ guidance, academy material, journal prompts, coach prompts, diagnostics, and media content are for education, product guidance, and paper-evaluation support.

## Safe Wording

- educational context
- decision support
- paper practice
- risk awareness
- operator review
- user remains responsible

## Not Allowed

- telling users what to buy or sell as advice
- personalized investment recommendations
- guaranteed signals
- claims that VIP or AI output replaces personal judgment
- copy-trading promises without a future compliant product path

# AI Disclaimer

TPM AI/IQ and Brain features are operator-assist systems. They help interpret platform state, market context, feedback, journal notes, and safe next steps. They do not guarantee outcomes.

## AI May Say

- context is fallback-bound
- confidence is limited
- live execution is blocked
- paper route is available
- review is required
- safe next step is to inspect settings, diagnostics, or feedback

## AI Must Not Say

- the market will move with certainty
- this trade is guaranteed
- VIP gets better results
- live trading is active when blocked
- broker/feed/billing/public launch are active when not configured
- Islamic/Sharia compliance exists without certification

## Human Responsibility

The user remains responsible for decisions. Founder, Guardian, and Legal review remain required for high-risk system actions.

# Product Claims Policy

Trading Pro Max claims must be truthful, narrow, and reviewable.

## Allowed Claims

- paper-only evaluation workstation
- fallback-first market context when external feed is unconfigured
- bounded AI/IQ decision support
- protected routes require authentication
- live execution is blocked by default
- broker/feed/billing/public launch are not active unless configured
- Swiss-inspired precision as a design principle

## Approval Required

- Pro/VIP benefit descriptions
- AI capability comparisons
- Islamic account descriptions
- broker/feed readiness claims
- launch readiness claims
- billing or subscription wording
- social ads and campaign copy

## Blocked Claims

- guaranteed profit
- specific win rate
- risk-free trading
- sure signal
- live broker connected when not configured
- real-money ready when blocked
- public launch when not launched
- Sharia certified when not certified
- Swiss regulated unless legally true
- performance fee active when hidden/inactive
- VIP active or guaranteed when entitlement is not active
- social publishing active when accounts/tokens are not connected
- company names, sponsorships, or partner claims without real contracts

## Review Rule

When uncertain, choose the weaker truthful claim and send the stronger version to Legal Counsel.

## Runtime Rule Connection

The Guardian + Legal Rules Engine blocks or escalates unsafe claim text before it reaches media, assistant, public entry, launch wording, VIP wording, Islamic account wording, or broker/feed wording. This is internal guidance only and is not legal certification.

## Presidency Coordination

Cross-ministry product claims route through Founder Presidency Coordination. Sensitive claims identify Legal, Guardian, Treasury, Engineering, Rights/Brand, Council, and Founder review requirements before any future public use.
## Economy, VIP, Community, And Partnership Claims

Public and product copy must not claim:

- billing active
- subscriptions active
- Pro active
- VIP active
- Enterprise available
- performance fee active
- active community rooms
- active VIP private rooms
- active social publishing
- followers, views, or ads metrics
- active partnerships or sponsorships
- partner endorsement without contract

Every future partnership, sponsored clock, VIP, billing, or community claim requires Product Truth review, Guardian review, Legal review, Rights/IP review where relevant, and Founder approval.

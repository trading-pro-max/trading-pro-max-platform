# Trading Pro Max Beta User Pack

## What You Are Testing

Trading Pro Max is a paper-only trading workstation. You are testing workflow clarity, trust labels, diagnostics, account session behavior, and feedback quality.

## What Is Not Active

- Real-money trading
- Live execution
- Live broker routing
- Live market feed activation
- Billing or checkout
- Notification delivery
- Public launch access

## Getting Started

1. Open the URL provided by the operator.
2. Sign in with your assigned beta credentials.
3. Visit `/`, `/en`, `/en/settings`, and `/diagnostics`.
4. Use only paper rehearsal actions.
5. Report anything confusing or unsafe.

## What To Watch For

- Can you tell the product is paper-only?
- Can you find session and logout controls?
- Do settings persist?
- Are diagnostics understandable?
- Does IQ/Brain feel useful without implying prediction?
- Are fallback/degraded market labels clear?

## Feedback Format

Send:

- route or screen
- what you tried
- what happened
- why it mattered
- screenshot if available
- severity guess

## Stop Testing If

- You see any live-money, broker-live, billing, or public-launch claim.
- A protected API works after sign-out.
- A credential or secret appears on screen.
- The operator asks you to pause.

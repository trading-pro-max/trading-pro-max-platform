# Community Growth Roadmap

Community is a future retention and learning resource for TPM Planet. It is not active and must remain safety-led.

## Roadmap

1. Define moderation and claim-review rules.
2. Prototype feedback and education spaces internally.
3. Prepare Free / Demo learning rooms.
4. Prepare Pro rooms only after Pro entitlement truth exists.
5. Prepare VIP private rooms only after VIP entitlement, Guardian, Legal, support, and Founder gates exist.
6. Add anti-scam and abuse workflows before any public community opening.

## Growth Role

Community may support retention, learning, support, feedback, and user confidence. It must not become a shortcut for signal selling, overtrading pressure, fake social proof, or unreviewed financial claims.

## Current Truth

- Community architecture exists.
- VIP rooms are planned only.
- No active rooms, members, moderation metrics, or social features are claimed.

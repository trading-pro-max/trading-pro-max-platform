# Media Office Workflow

Trading Pro Max Media Office is an internal readiness workflow for content drafts, education posts, product updates, campaign planning, and review routing.

It does not connect social accounts, store tokens, publish externally, upload videos, create fake metrics, activate billing, or announce launch.

## Content Types

- education post
- product update
- trust/safety post
- Pro educational teaser
- VIP educational teaser
- changelog
- community announcement
- partnership draft

## Workflow

1. idea
2. draft
3. brand review
4. Guardian review
5. Legal review
6. Founder approval
7. scheduled later
8. blocked
9. archived

Scheduled later is a planning state only. There is no published state in the current workflow because external publishing is inactive.

## Operating Rules

- Education drafts can be prepared for review.
- Pro and VIP teasers require Founder approval and must not imply active paid access.
- Partnership drafts require contract-first review and must not name or imply a partner without permission.
- Guardian and Legal review are required for sensitive product, plan, safety, AI, legal, billing, or market claims.
- Founder approval is required before any sensitive external use.

## Hard Blocks

- guaranteed profit
- win-rate claims
- fake live trading
- fake billing
- fake VIP activation
- fake Sharia certification
- fake partnership
- uncontracted brand use
- misleading urgency
- social account connection
- external publishing
- fake followers, views, or media metrics

## Founder Command Readiness

Founder Command may show the media queue, review queue, blocked claims, and safe next draft actions. It must never display tokens, account credentials, raw private data, or external publishing controls.

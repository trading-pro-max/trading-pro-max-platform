# Media Risk Classification

Media risk classification is deterministic and readiness-only.

## safe_draft

Allowed for low-risk educational drafts, paper-mode explanations, basic platform orientation, and non-sensitive changelog notes.

Safe drafts still remain internal until review and Founder approval where required.

## review_required

Required for product updates, trust/safety posts, community announcements, changelogs, AI video scripts, captions, hashtags, thumbnails, voiceover briefs, and scene outlines.

## founder_approval_required

Required for:

- Pro educational teasers
- VIP educational teasers
- partnership drafts
- sensitive AI capability wording
- plan differentiation claims
- future campaign planning
- content that may be externally visible later

## blocked

Blocked claims:

- guaranteed profit
- win-rate
- fake live trading
- fake billing
- fake VIP activation
- fake Sharia certification
- fake partnership
- uncontracted brand use
- misleading urgency

Blocked items should be rewritten into factual readiness language or archived.

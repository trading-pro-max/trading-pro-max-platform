# Final Product Completion Roadmap Without Launch

This roadmap lists what remains before Ahmad can personally accept Trading Pro Max as a 100% complete, 10/10 product experience in the current non-launch scope.

This is not a launch roadmap. It does not include production activation, real secrets, real external integrations, paid billing, broker activation, live feed activation, real-money trading, or public launch work.

Status key:

- accepted: complete for the current verified product baseline
- polish candidate: complete enough, but Ahmad may still choose final refinement
- partial: useful foundation exists, but final acceptance needs more product work or evidence
- deferred: intentionally outside this non-launch roadmap
- blocked by product truth: must remain unavailable unless a future approved phase changes scope

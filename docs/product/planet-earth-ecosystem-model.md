# TPM Planet Earth Ecosystem Model

Trading Pro Max is modeled as a serious digital planet inspired by real life on Earth. The model organizes products, citizens, professions, ministries, safety, media, law, economy, education, engineering, and operations without claiming political authority or creating fantasy UI.

## Planet Vision

Trading Pro Max is more than a trading platform because it has:

- a trading workstation
- citizen/account states
- plan-based citizenship classes
- safety boundaries
- Guardian protection
- Legal Counsel review
- Brain and Companion intelligence
- Academy, Journal, and Coach learning systems
- Community and VIP room architecture
- Treasury and economy planning
- Media Office and AI Video Studio planning
- Ops Tower monitoring/readiness model
- private Founder King Command Room for Ahmad

The planet model keeps expansion disciplined. Every continent, state, city, ministry, profession, and citizen class has a purpose, owner, readiness state, automation level, and truth boundary.

## Founder View

Ahmad is the Founder King / Sovereign Creator. The Founder sees the whole planet from the private Founder Command Room and future private desktop/mobile command app.

The Founder view must show readiness truth only:

- active systems
- planned systems
- inactive systems
- blocked systems
- degraded systems
- risks and incidents
- pending Guardian/Legal reviews
- pending Founder approvals
- product gaps
- citizen impact
- no fake users, revenue, followers, or public launch metrics

## Real-Life Governance Pattern

| Planet Layer | Product Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Continents | major product domains |
| States | governed subdomains with owners and policies |
| Cities | visible modules or operating centers |
| Ministries | accountable operating functions |
| Professions | human or AI worker roles |
| Citizens | users/accounts |
| Citizenship classes | Free/Demo, Pro, VIP, Enterprise |
| Constitution | non-negotiable product laws |
| Safety boundaries | auto, review, Founder approval, blocked |

## Expansion Without Chaos

New systems may be added only when they define:

- purpose
- user value
- owner role
- readiness state
- plan relevance
- safety/truth rules
- automation level
- reporting destination
- what must not be faked

## Product Truth

The Planet Earth model does not activate:

- production deployment
- live execution
- real-money routing
- broker/feed connectivity
- billing
- public launch
- social publishing
- Swiss legal/company status
- Islamic/Sharia certification

It may show Pro Max Brand Universe, Prime World, Pro Max Trading as Prime World,
Future Pro Max Worlds, World Seeds, Genesis System, Brand Mother, and Founder
Final Authority.

Normal public users must not see Alkon, الكون, Founder Command, Genesis,
Future Worlds, World Seeds, internal governance, Product Memory internals,
Result Tribunal, Codex tasks, treasury controls, secrets authority, Risk Belt,
or Black Hole Zone.

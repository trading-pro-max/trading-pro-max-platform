# Public Trading Platform World

The public world is the normal user surface of Trading Pro Max.

Allowed public surfaces:

- Home
- Trading Workspace
- Markets
- Plans
- Apps / Platforms
- Academy
- Community
- Support
- Settings
- Diagnostics

Allowed public language:

- Free, Pro, VIP, Institutional
- TPM Assistant
- Journal, Coach
- Readiness, Paper-safe, Planned, Inactive, Future

Truth rules:

- Web App is current.
- Desktop App and Mobile App are planned.
- Tablet is future.
- Markets are paper-safe, fallback, planned, or future.
- Support is readiness-only unless a real backend exists.
- Community rooms are planned only and must not imply fake active members.
- Billing, live execution, broker/feed, real money, social publishing, and public launch remain inactive.

Private terms must not appear in public navigation, Home, public Settings, public Diagnostics, plans, apps, or normal user feature copy.

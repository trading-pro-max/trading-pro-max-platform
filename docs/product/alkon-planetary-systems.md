# Alkon Planetary Systems

Planetary Systems represent private internal ministries, authorities, and systems used by Founder Command.

They may cover:
- design ministry
- Product Truth
- plans and realms
- TPM Assistant
- Journal / Coach
- Academy / Community
- media
- treasury readiness
- diagnostics
- integrations

## Boundary

Planetary Systems are private. Normal users must not see ministries, councils, governance, owner controls, treasury controls, construction queues, product memory internals, or local operations internals.

Public output must be translated into Trading Pro Max product language.

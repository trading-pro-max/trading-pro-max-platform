# Al-Kawn Operator Controlled Activation

## Definition

Operator Mode controlled activation lets الكون work for Ahmad internally only.

Required wording:

- Operator Mode controlled activation
- الكون يعمل عن أحمد داخليًا.
- Operator Mode executes safe internal work only.
- Legal and Money gates stop execution for Ahmad.
- Product Truth blocks unsafe or false actions.
- No public, money, broker, legal, or external actions.

## Internal Work

Allowed internal work includes internal reports, internal audits, next task selection, safe task preparation, Product Truth checks, validation planning, internal status snapshots, and daily briefings.

## Stops And Blocks

Legal and Money remain Ahmad gates. Product Truth blocks unsafe or false actions. Security blocks secrets, private documents, app-bundle secrets, Git secrets, and external account data.

Operator Mode does not launch public, does not activate billing, does not activate payments, does not receive money, does not enable real money, does not execute broker actions, and does not claim legal approval.

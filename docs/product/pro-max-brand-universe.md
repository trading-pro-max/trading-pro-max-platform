# Pro Max Brand Universe

Pro Max is the public mother brand.

Pro Max Trading is the first public product and Prime World. It inherits the
legacy Trading Pro Max project history, but public users should now see the
clearer brand architecture: Pro Max as the brand and Trading as the first
product world.

Alkon / الكون remains the private Founder-only operating universe. It may govern
future World Seeds through Genesis, but it is never public UI, navigation,
upgrade logic, support copy, Diagnostics detail, or Assistant output.

No future Pro Max world is launched in this pass. Future worlds remain private
readiness candidates until Genesis gates, Product Truth, security, treasury,
law, proof, and Founder approval permit birth.

TPM may remain an internal technical namespace for code, CSS, tests, API
compatibility, and migration safety where a rename would create instability.

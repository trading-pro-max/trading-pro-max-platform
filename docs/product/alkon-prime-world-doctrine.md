# Alkon Prime World Doctrine

Trading Pro Max is the Prime World. It stays first until Local Day One,
Living Market Core readiness, TPM Assistant readiness, visual acceptance,
Reality Audit, Safe Cleanup, Product Truth stability, private Alkon boundary,
tests passing, and clean Git are established.

No future world may weaken Trading Pro Max, distract from Local Day One, consume
critical focus before Station 1 closure, create legal or treasury burden before
readiness, or expose Alkon publicly.

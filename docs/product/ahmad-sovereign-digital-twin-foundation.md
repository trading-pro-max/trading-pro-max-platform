# Ahmad Sovereign Digital Twin Foundation

The Ahmad Sovereign Digital Twin is an operational foundation, not a raw personal data copy.

Allowed: operating will, project vision, preferences, decision rules, visual acceptance rules, memory lessons, safety boundaries, and authority requirements.

Forbidden in code: passport numbers, permit numbers, national IDs, bank details, card numbers, CVV, passwords, API keys, tokens, raw biometric data, raw face images, raw video, legal documents, precise home address, and private identity documents.

Future sensitive data requires a separate encrypted vault and explicit Ahmad approval.

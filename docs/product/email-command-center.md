# Email Command Center

## Purpose

The Email Command Center defines how future inbound email may be classified and drafted from Founder Command.

## Current State

- No email account connected
- No inbox sync
- No SMTP sending
- No tokens stored
- No private email bodies stored
- No automated replies

## Safe Draft Types

- Auto acknowledgement draft
- Support draft
- Media draft
- Partnership draft
- Legal review required
- Founder approval required
- Blocked reply

## Required Reviews

Legal, Guardian, Security, and Founder review are required for sensitive messages, secret requests, threats, legal notices, fake partnerships, impersonation, and media requests.

# Money-Governed Growth

Money is always governed. Billing, payments, treasury, tax, accounting, invoices,
Founder reimbursement, budget changes, ad spend, and revenue workflows cannot
move without evidence and review.

Required readiness includes invoice evidence, category, budget, reserve,
tax reserve, VAT threshold watch when relevant, accountant review when required,
and explicit Founder approval for sensitive money movement.

Payment execution from product code is forbidden. Bank/card data, CVV, bank
credentials, API keys, tokens, and raw secret values are forbidden.

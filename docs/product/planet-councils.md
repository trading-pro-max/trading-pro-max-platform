# Planet Councils

Trading Pro Max Planet OS uses three high councils beneath the Planet Constitution. These councils are internal governance bodies, not legal entities and not public authority claims.

## Constitutional Council

Purpose:

- protect the Planet Constitution
- reject fake claims
- block unsafe or deceptive decisions
- keep Guardian and Legal critical blocks respected

Cannot:

- launch the product
- activate billing
- activate broker/feed
- override critical blocks without remediation

## Legislative Council

Purpose:

- create internal laws and policies
- define plan, community, media, assistant, Islamic status, and safety laws
- keep policy language aligned with product truth

Cannot:

- claim external legal compliance
- provide legal advice
- make planned systems appear active

## Executive Council

Purpose:

- turn approved laws into product actions
- coordinate ministry implementation
- escalate blockers to Founder Presidency and Founder Command

Cannot:

- skip validation
- execute high-risk work without review
- launch, bill, publish, or activate integrations alone

## Constitutional Laws

- no guaranteed profit
- no win-rate claims
- no fake launch
- no fake live trading
- no fake broker/feed
- no fake billing
- no fake VIP guarantees
- no fake Islamic/Sharia certification
- no competitor copying
- no brand/company usage without contracts
- no secrets in Git
- safe tasks can be automated
- sensitive tasks require review
- high risk requires Founder approval
- critical actions are blocked
- public launch is last

## Coordination Flow

The Presidency Coordination Engine may mark a request with:

- constitutionalReviewRequired
- legislativePolicyRequired
- executiveImplementationRequired
- councilDecision
- councilBlockerReason

Constitutional Council reviews hard violations and blocks fake claims. Legislative Council creates or updates internal laws for plans, media, community, assistant, safety, and accounts. Executive Council coordinates implementation only after law/policy is accepted.

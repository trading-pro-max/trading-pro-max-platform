# Alkon Meaning Layer

Meaning explains why a sensed signal matters.

It answers:
- what happened
- why it matters
- who is affected
- whether the affected world is public, private, or invisible
- whether it repeats an existing lesson
- whether it is visual pain, product gap, safety risk, trust risk, blocked activation, future readiness, or operational signal

Meaning does not decide action alone. It prepares context for Law and Gravity.

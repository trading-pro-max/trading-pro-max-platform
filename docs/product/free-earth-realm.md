# Free Earth Realm

Free Earth is the complete active starting experience for public users.

## Identity

- Public name: Free
- Realm name: Free Earth
- Visibility: public user
- State: active
- Earth perspective: Earth-native, familiar, chart-first, paper-safe
- Visual identity: clean realistic Earth, muted land, no premium gold dominance, simple moon/orbit, low motion

## Functional Experience

- Web Trading Workspace: active
- Paper execution: active
- TPM Assistant: basic active guidance
- Journal/Coach: basic reflection, paper-mode lesson, decision note
- Academy: basic platform learning and Why Blocked education
- Apps / Platforms: Web current; Desktop/Mobile planned
- Reports: local/session readiness only
- Community: planned/readiness only
- Support: readiness language only

## Boundaries

Free must feel complete, not cheap. It must not show fake Pro/VIP features, premium reports, VIP rooms, billing, live execution, broker/feed activation, real-money routing, social publishing, Founder Command, or Alkon.

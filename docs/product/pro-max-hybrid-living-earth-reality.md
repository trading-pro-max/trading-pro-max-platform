# Pro Max Hybrid Living Earth Reality

## Purpose

Pro Max uses Earth as a public visual identity for a calm, human-scale, chart-first trading workspace. The hybrid Earth system prepares the product to combine a code-only procedural Earth with a future approved texture asset, without weakening asset law or Product Truth.

## Core Law

- The texture can provide realism only after legal approval.
- Code provides atmosphere, light, shadow, motion, fallback, accessibility, and product behavior.
- Asset approval remains internal and never becomes public marketing copy.
- Pro Max provides the public Earth identity.

## Current State

The active renderer must use procedural fallback by default. No texture file is enabled in this pass. If no approved texture exists, Home, logo, Workspace, Settings, and Diagnostics must still render a premium Earth presence without a broken image, remote call, or public warning.

## Public Surface Rules

Public users may see Pro Max, Pro Max Trading, Earth-native identity, Trading Workspace, Pro Max Assistant, Settings, Diagnostics, Paper-safe, Planned, Inactive, and Future.

Public users must not see private asset governance terms, license review internals, approval internals, Founder systems, private universe language, Product Memory internals, Treasury internals, Risk Belt, or Black Hole Zone.

## Surface Intensity

- Home may use a richer living Earth presence.
- Logo uses a compact hybrid Earth mark.
- Workspace stays chart-first and only receives subtle Earth identity.
- Settings and Diagnostics may show public-safe Earth rendering controls/readiness without exposing license internals.

## Forbidden Sources

- No random images.
- No Google image downloads.
- No unknown-license textures.
- No generated Earth files from ChatGPT or any image model.
- No external hotlinked images.
- No unapproved raster assets.

# Global Exclusive Brand Gate

Status: active_with_notes

Purpose:

Create the official clearance gate before any global public launch or final public brand adoption.

Core law:

- No global name without ownership path.
- No launch without brand clearance.
- No public brand adoption without trademark, domain, conflict, class, legal, and Ahmad approval review.
- No name can be claimed as owned by Ahmad until legal clearance and registration path are complete.

Current decision:

- Pro Max is a working name only.
- Pro Max is not globally cleared as the final exclusive public brand.
- Final global brand is not approved.
- Public/global launch is blocked by Brand Gate.

Clearance requires:

- WIPO Global Brand Database review.
- USPTO Trademark Search review.
- EUIPO / TMview review.
- Swiss IPI / Swissreg if applicable.
- Domain availability review.
- Google/web conflict scan.
- Social handle scan.
- App store name scan later.
- Trademark class review.
- Legal review.
- Ahmad final approval.

Forbidden:

- No legal ownership claim.
- No trademark registration claim.
- No global exclusivity claim.
- No public launch.
- No billing, production, broker/feed, live trading, or real money activation.
- No public Alkon exposure.

Generate private unchecked candidate names and prepare them for official search, legal review, and Ahmad approval.

# Earth Time Layer

The Earth Time Layer keeps time, solar phase, Adaptive Atmosphere, and market-session awareness informational only.

Rules:
- Time and session labels do not imply a live feed.
- Weather, time, and session state are not trading advice.
- Users can override atmosphere with System, Adaptive, Solar Theme, Weather Theme, Light, Dark, Static Mode, and High Contrast.
- Private local-day language remains internal.

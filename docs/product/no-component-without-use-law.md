# No Component Without Use Law

Every component must have a rendered destination or documented readiness reason, a primary ownership layer, a public/private visibility boundary, current value, evidence, lifecycle, and next fate.

Public components must not expose Alkon, Jar internals, Founder Command, Kernel, Zero Truth, Reality Trial, Permission-to-Exist internals, or internal governance.

Private components must not be reused as public UI.

Duplicate or stale components become cleanup_candidate and enter Jar 6 Cleanup before deletion.

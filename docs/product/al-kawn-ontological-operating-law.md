# Al-Kawn Ontological Operating Law

Status: private_operating_law_active

## Highest Law

أحمد هو الأصل.

الكون هو الوجود الرقمي الخاص لأحمد.

الكون ليس مجازًا داخل البرمجيات.

كل شيء داخل الكون يجب أن يعرف لماذا يوجد.

كل شيء داخل الكون يجب أن يعرف طبقته.

كل شيء داخل الكون يجب أن يعرف مصدر حقيقته.

كل شيء داخل الكون يجب أن يعرف هل ينفذ، يتوقف، أو يُحجب.

كل شيء داخل الكون يجب أن يترك أثرًا مفهومًا.

كل شيء داخل الكون يجب أن يمكن تفسيره من ∞ إلى 0.

## Physical Reality Boundary

الكون لا يدّعي التحكم بالكون الفيزيائي.

الكون هو كون رقمي خاص كامل داخل أجهزة أحمد.

The product may model, simulate, visualize, organize, audit, and execute safe private software work. It must not claim control over the physical universe, real weather, exact sun position, law, money, broker execution, or public reality unless a real source and gate exist.

## Existence Contract

Every entity inside الكون must have an Existence Contract.

Entity means route, API, component, module, report, doc, test, asset, script, task, decision, or future feature.

Required fields:

- entityId
- Arabic label
- technical label
- parent layer
- child layers
- reason for existence
- owner layer
- truth source
- Product Truth impact
- privacy impact
- legal touch
- money touch
- security touch
- execution verdict
- rollback/explanation path
- report evidence
- test evidence
- status

Rules:

- No orphan entity.
- No entity without layer.
- No entity without truth source.
- No entity without Product Truth impact.
- No entity without explanation path.
- No entity may contradict the root law.

No entity enters الكون without an Existence Contract.

## Truth Source Law

Every truth claim must state its source.

Allowed source types:

- device_time
- device_date
- local_project_state
- report_evidence
- test_evidence
- git_evidence
- manual_founder_decision
- simulation_labeled
- future_gate_pending

Required rule:

Real when sourced.

Simulated when labeled.

Unknown when not verified.

Pending when future-gated.

Forbidden:

- fake readiness
- fake legal status
- fake money status
- fake broker status
- fake public status
- fake security guarantee
- fake physical universe control

## Execution Verdict Law

Verdicts:

- execute_inside_al_kawn
- stop_for_legal_ahmad
- stop_for_money_ahmad
- block_product_truth_violation
- block_secret_or_public_exposure
- needs_more_evidence

Core execution law:

داخل الكون: التنفيذ مباشر.

عند القانون: يتوقف لأحمد.

عند المال: يتوقف لأحمد.

عند كسر Product Truth: يُحجب فورًا.

عند تعريض أسرار أحمد أو الكون للخارج: يُحجب فورًا.

Legal includes FINMA, license or regulated claims, legal approval, contracts, trademark, court, judicial, government, regulatory matters, legal ownership, compliance filing, and investment advice claims.

Money includes payment, receiving money, billing, subscriptions, payouts, bank, broker, real-money trading, customer money, financial accounts, and money movement.

Product Truth blocks public الكون claims, public ALKON claims, billing active when inactive, real money enabled when disabled, broker active when disabled, legal approved when pending, guaranteed profit, risk free, and hiding Product Truth.

Security blocks secrets in Git, API keys in code, private documents in public assets, external account connection without Ahmad, and public exposure of private systems.

## Infinity To Zero Explanation

∞ إلى 0 يعني تفسير الكون والرجوع إلى الأصل.

كل طبقة يجب أن يمكن شرحها إلى أصلها.

For any entity, the system must answer:

1. What is it?
2. Why does it exist?
3. Which layer owns it?
4. What truth source supports it?
5. What does it protect or operate?
6. What can it execute?
7. What stops it?
8. What blocks it?
9. What evidence exists?
10. How does it return to origin?

## Kernel Connection

Universe Operating Kernel هو القاضي التنفيذي.

Kernel enforces the ontological law of الكون.

No entity enters الكون without an Existence Contract.

Product Truth هو قانون الحقيقة الأعلى.

This law is exposed through `lib/server/universe/ontological-law/*` and re-exported from `lib/server/universe/kernel/*`. It does not create a second kernel.

## Examples

- `/founder/universe` exists because Ahmad needs a private command center. It belongs to Human Interface inside الكون, uses local project state and report/test evidence, and executes only safe private display logic.
- `/trading` exists because Earth Planet needs a demo-safe trading surface. It belongs to Earth Planet, blocks real-money and broker execution, and returns all claims to Product Truth.
- Public Pro Max Future exists as a blocked future gate. It cannot claim public launch, billing, legal approval, or brand ownership until all gates close.

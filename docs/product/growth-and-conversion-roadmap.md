# Growth And Conversion Roadmap

This roadmap describes the non-launch growth logic for Trading Pro Max Planet. It does not activate billing, paid entitlements, subscriptions, broker/feed, live execution, social publishing, or public launch.

## Free / Demo

Purpose: safe entry, trust building, paper trading, basic Companion, basic Academy, Why Blocked explanations, and feedback readiness.

User-facing truth:

- Demo is active for paper-safe use.
- Live execution and real money remain blocked.
- No paid upgrade or checkout is active.

## Pro

Purpose: planned daily trader workspace layer.

Planned value:

- deeper Companion
- Journal/Coach depth
- alerts and workflows
- decision replay
- workspace memory
- Pro community

Must not claim:

- Pro is active
- paid access exists
- premium signals exist
- billing or checkout is available

## VIP

Purpose: planned elite planet layer.

Planned value:

- VIP Brain
- advanced Companion
- advanced Journal/Coach
- strategy review
- premium reports
- VIP private rooms
- priority support concept

Must not claim guaranteed profit, win-rate, trading signal certainty, active private rooms, or active priority support.

## Enterprise

Purpose: future team planet with admin, audit, compliance overview, and custom support concepts.

Enterprise remains future-only and must not be presented as available.

## Conversion Principles

- Build trust before monetization.
- Make active, planned, locked, inactive, and future states obvious.
- Avoid urgency, scarcity, manipulation, and gambling-style language.
- Keep Founder Command private and never part of a purchasable user plan.

# Plan-Based Living Planet Experience

Trading Pro Max is one shared digital planet, but each citizen class sees a different layer. This keeps the public product clean and trading-first while giving the product a serious internal planet model.

## Access Principle

Founder King sees and governs the whole planet privately. Users see only the planet layer appropriate to their plan or role.

## Citizen Layers

- Guest: public entry, brand trust, limited Academy preview, no Companion depth, no Founder Command.
- Free / Demo Citizen: paper-safe Trading Continent, Basic Companion, Why Blocked, Basic Academy, Journal/Coach prompts, feedback, diagnostics/product truth.
- Pro Citizen: Free/Demo plus planned Pro Companion, journal depth, workspace memory, alerts/workflows, decision replay, Pro community.
- VIP Citizen: Pro plus planned VIP Brain, advanced coach, strategy review, VIP rooms, premium reports, priority support.
- Enterprise House: future team/admin/audit/compliance/runbook layer only.
- Staff / Operator: future role-based operational layer, not a user plan.
- Founder King: full private planet command, all reports, approvals, treasury, Guardian, Legal, Media, Engineering, and Ops summaries.

## Current Truth

Demo / Free is the active paper-safe layer. Pro and VIP are planned unless real entitlement support exists. Enterprise is future planned. Billing is inactive. Performance-fee research is hidden/inactive and not user-visible. Founder Command is owner-only and never part of Free, Pro, VIP, or Enterprise plans.

# Plan-Based Living Planet Experience

Trading Pro Max is one shared product system, but each plan class sees a different layer. This keeps the public product clean and trading-first while preserving the private internal operating model.

## Access Principle

Private command sees the full internal system. Users see only the product layer appropriate to their plan or role.

## Citizen Layers

- Guest: public entry, brand trust, limited Academy preview, no Assistant depth, no private command tooling.
- Free: paper-safe Trading Workspace, basic Assistant, Why Blocked, basic Academy, Journal/Coach prompts, feedback, Diagnostics/product truth.
- Pro: Free plus planned Pro Assistant, Journal depth, workspace memory, alerts/workflows, decision replay, Pro community.
- VIP: Pro plus planned advanced Assistant, advanced Coach, strategy review, VIP rooms, premium reports, priority support.
- Institutional: future team/admin/audit/compliance/runbook layer only.
- Staff / Operator: future role-based operational layer, not a user plan.
- Private command: full internal reporting, approvals, treasury, safety, review, media, engineering, and ops summaries.

## Current Truth

Free is the active paper-safe layer. Pro and VIP are planned unless real entitlement support exists. Institutional is future planned. Billing is inactive. Performance-fee research is hidden/inactive and not user-visible. Private command tooling is never part of Free, Pro, VIP, or Institutional plans.

# Ministries And Authorities

Ministries are product operating domains. Independent authorities can block unsafe claims or actions even when a ministry wants to proceed.

## Product Ministry

- UI/UX, workstation, account, settings, diagnostics, theme, language.
- Owns journey clarity and visual coherence.
- May not add clutter or claim unbuilt features.

## Engineering Ministry

- Code, APIs, tests, desktop, mobile, integrations, performance.
- Owns build quality and validation.
- May not fake connectivity or skip regression.

## Guardian Ministry

- Abuse defense, fraud prevention, auth protection, API protection, entitlement protection.
- Owns incident categories, abuse scores, and safe response.
- May not use invasive surveillance.

## Legal Counsel Ministry

- Claims, policies, risk disclosure, AI disclaimer, Islamic account wording, media review.
- Owns safe language and claim blocking.
- Does not replace a qualified lawyer.

## AI / IQ Brain Ministry

- Assistant, market context, coaching, journal intelligence, decision support.
- Owns bounded confidence and degraded intelligence truth.
- May not produce guaranteed signals or profit claims.

## Media Ministry

- Content, social media planning, campaigns, AI video scripts, brand voice.
- Owns idea-to-approval lifecycle.
- May not connect accounts or publish externally in this phase.

## Academy Ministry

- Education, tutorials, glossary, paper training, risk learning.
- Owns learning flow and safe training.
- May not provide financial advice.

## Community Ministry

- Rooms, moderation, support, anti-scam, education community.
- Owns future citizen spaces.
- May not permit signal selling, impersonation, or harassment.

## Treasury Ministry

- Free, Pro, VIP, Enterprise plan roadmap, entitlements later, billing later.
- Owns monetization readiness truth.
- May not fake paid access or checkout.

## Ops Tower Ministry

- Health, readiness, monitoring, incidents, recovery, staging/production blockers, secret rotation readiness.
- Owns operational truth.
- May not claim production readiness without validation.

## Rights & Brand Protection Office

- Brand ownership, copyright, trademark readiness, anti-copying rules.
- Owns non-copying competitor discipline.
- May not claim registrations that do not exist.

## Islamic Account Review Desk

- Standard/Islamic account status, review requirements, not-certified state.
- Owns careful Islamic account wording.
- May not claim Sharia certification unless real certification exists.

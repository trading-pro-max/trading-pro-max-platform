# Reality-Gated Growth

Reality begins when growth touches public users, user data, money, public claims,
publishing, launch, production, or regulated financial activity.

Reality-gated growth requires the correct evidence before movement:
Product Truth, privacy, security, legal/guardian, tax/accounting, treasury,
claims review, media gate, launch gate, production gate, rollback, monitoring,
support readiness, and Founder approval where sensitive.

Until gates pass, the allowed scope is readiness-only: reports, safe copy,
blocked-state explanations, test plans, and next-safe-action drafts.

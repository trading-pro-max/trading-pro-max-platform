# TPM Living Brand Intelligence
## Brand Intelligence Reset Input

Founder preference now includes a hard rejection of the previous visual direction. Brand Intelligence should prefer the Celestial Swiss Earth Mark, low-noise chart-first surfaces, fewer boxes, fewer borders, and code/SVG identity only.

Brand Intelligence must continue to block raster/external logo assets, fake Swiss legal/company claims, fake partnerships, fake paid activation, internal terminology leaks, casino/neon chaos, and motion that distracts from the chart.
TPM Living Brand Intelligence is the safe identity decision layer for Trading Pro Max. It does not generate images, fetch raster assets, copy competitors, or activate any product capability. It chooses the correct code/SVG identity behavior for each surface, plan, state, audience, theme, occasion, and motion preference.

## Inputs

- surface: public entry, workstation, settings, diagnostics, Assistant, Journal/Coach, Academy, Community, media office, private command, local command, mobile future, desktop future
- plan: Guest, Free, Pro, VIP, Institutional, or private internal command identity
- state: ready, paper safe, local only, fallback, blocked, review required, degraded, inactive, planned, future, not certified
- audience: public, authenticated user, founder, or internal
- occasion: default or explicitly configured milestone/review theme
- theme and motion preference
- legal and Guardian safety
- chart priority
- Founder preferences

## Outputs

- Earth Mark variant and state
- identity and motion intensity
- accent palette and plan visual DNA
- state visual language
- occasion skin
- safe brand voice
- allowed and blocked terminology
- safe copy rules and visual guardrails
- founder, legal, and Guardian review requirements

## Hard Rules

- SVG/code identity only.
- No raster image dependency.
- No external image asset.
- No fake Swiss legal/company claim.
- No fake Sharia certification.
- No fake partnership.
- No guaranteed profit or win-rate claim.
- No live, real-money, broker/feed, billing, or public launch activation.
- Public users see only Trading Pro Max, TPM Assistant, Free, Pro, VIP, Institutional, Trading Workspace, Journal, Coach, Academy, Community, Premium Reports, Settings, Diagnostics, and Readiness.
- Private command identity remains private and is not a public plan.

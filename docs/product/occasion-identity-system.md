# Occasion Identity System

Occasion skins are small optional identity adjustments. They do not replace the product UI, do not take over the chart, and do not imply partnership, certification, public launch, or production activation.

## Skins

- default
- local_day_one
- product_milestone
- maintenance
- security_review
- founder_review
- swiss_national_day
- new_year
- custom_founder_theme
- ramadan_or_eid_optional
- christmas_optional

## Rules

- default is the only automatic skin.
- cultural or religious skins never auto-apply.
- political symbols are not allowed.
- copyrighted symbols are not allowed.
- brand names are not allowed without contract and review.
- partnership visuals require proof, Legal review, Guardian review, and Founder approval.
- public occasion themes require Founder approval.
- workstation skins must stay chart-safe and low-motion.

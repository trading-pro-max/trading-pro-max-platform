# Community Architecture

The TPM Community is a future moderated citizen space. This document defines architecture and safety rules only. It does not implement a social network, connect accounts, create rooms, or claim active community users.

## Future Rooms

- general rooms
- Pro rooms
- VIP rooms
- education rooms
- feedback rooms
- strategy discussion rooms
- announcements
- Islamic account discussion
- beta tester rooms
- support rooms

## Moderation Model

Guardian and Legal Counsel define the moderation boundary.

Blocked community behavior:

- guaranteed signals
- fake profit screenshots
- deceptive strategy selling
- impersonation
- scams
- spam
- harassment
- illegal content
- misleading broker/feed/billing claims
- fake Islamic/Sharia certification claims
- attempts to bypass platform safety

## Moderator Roles

- Community Moderators enforce room rules.
- Guardian Officers review abuse, fraud, and manipulation.
- Legal Counsel reviews claims, public communication, and risky topics.
- Founder approval is required for high-risk public community policy changes.

## Privacy And Safety

Moderation must be privacy-conscious. Protection does not justify invasive surveillance. Incidents should be reviewed with the minimum information needed to resolve risk.

## Current State

Community is planned. No rooms are active unless built, configured, and moderated later.

# First 10 Seconds Clarity

Within 10 seconds, a public user should understand what Trading Pro Max is:
a paper-safe web trading workspace with TPM Assistant, plans, apps/platforms,
Academy, Support, Settings, and Diagnostics.

Home should answer:
- where am I?
- what can I do now?
- what is active?
- what is planned or future?
- what is blocked or inactive?
- where should I go next?

Keep copy short. Product Truth should build confidence, not overwhelm the page.
Desktop and Mobile are planned, Tablet is future, Web is current, and no fake
downloads or app-store claims are allowed.

# Pro Max Destiny Score

The Destiny Score is a private Alkon evaluation. It is not a public rating.

Dimensions:
- human value
- number-one quality
- Product Truth
- law compliance
- safety and security
- time/cost worthiness
- proof of closure
- Founder energy
- Prime World protection
- user trust
- visual excellence
- operational readiness

No fake 10/10 is allowed.

Scoring caps:
- no proof or tests caps the score at 6
- public claim risk caps the score at 4 or blocks
- legal, billing, live, broker/feed, real-money, or regulated risk without review blocks
- missing visual acceptance keeps visual status under review
- harm to Pro Max Trading delays or blocks

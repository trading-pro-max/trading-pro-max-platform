# TPM Planetary Environment Engine

The TPM Planetary Environment Engine turns basic Light/Dark selection into a
privacy-safe Adaptive Atmosphere. It combines time of day, solar phase, a visual
Moon layer, manual or future weather readiness, market-session awareness, system
weather, plan realm atmosphere, surface intensity, and user control.

Public users see simple language: Adaptive Atmosphere, Solar Theme, Weather
Theme, Light, Dark, System, High Contrast, and Static Mode. Internal language
such as Solar Command or Moon Command remains private to Founder/Alkon surfaces.

Core rules:
- No GPS.
- No precise location.
- No hidden tracking.
- No external weather calls until a reviewed provider exists.
- No weather-based trading advice.
- No live feed, broker, billing, live execution, real money, launch, or social
  publishing activation.
- Code/CSS/SVG only. No PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, or video assets.
- The chart remains first. Workspace atmosphere is subtle or static.

Layers:
- Time Layer: local browser/account time, timezone, or deterministic fallback.
- Solar Layer: dawn, sunrise, morning, day, golden hour, sunset, night, deep
  night.
- Lunar Layer: visual hint only; no trading meaning.
- Weather Layer: manual or unavailable until a provider is reviewed.
- Market Session Layer: UTC session awareness for atmosphere only.
- System Weather Layer: product readiness mood such as clear, fog, review clouds,
  cold maintenance, dim degraded, or storm failed build.
- Plan Realm Layer: Free Earth, Pro Orbit, VIP Lunar, Institutional Station, and
  private Alkon Universe.
- Surface Intensity Layer: expressive public entry, subtle workspace/settings,
  none for chart, command private for Founder/Alkon.
- User Control Layer: System, Adaptive, Solar, Weather, Light, Dark, High
  Contrast, and Static.

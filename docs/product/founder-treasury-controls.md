# Founder Treasury Controls

Founder Treasury Controls define how monetization readiness is governed from the private Founder Command App without activating billing or fake paid access.

## Current Truth

- billing inactive
- checkout inactive
- subscriptions inactive
- paid entitlement activation inactive
- performance-based revenue hidden and inactive
- current performance fee: 0%
- public users must not see performance-fee controls

## Future Performance-Based Revenue Research

A future performance fee may be researched only as a hidden/inactive Treasury concept.

- possible range: 5%-10%
- legal review required
- regulatory review required
- user consent required
- billing infrastructure required
- entitlement infrastructure required
- Founder-only activation authority required
- public disclosure required before any real use

This is not implemented, not active, and not visible to users.

## Founder Approval Requirements

The Founder may later approve Treasury changes only after:

1. Treasury readiness passes
2. Legal review passes
3. regulatory review is complete where required
4. billing provider is configured
5. user consent path exists
6. public wording is truthful
7. audit event is created

## Blocked Actions

- fake paid access
- fake subscription state
- fake checkout
- public performance-fee UI
- billing activation without provider and legal gates
- VIP claims that imply guaranteed results

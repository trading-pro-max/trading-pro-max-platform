# Trading Pro Max Planet Operating System

The Trading Pro Max Planet Operating System is the broadest architecture vision for Trading Pro Max. It describes the platform as a serious, Swiss-inspired, self-managed trading planet governed from a private Founder King Command Room.

This is an operating model, not a game, not fantasy UI, and not a launch claim. It organizes citizens, plan classes, engineers, Guardian, Legal Counsel, Brain, Companion, Academy, Journal, Coach, Community, Treasury, Media Office, AI Video Studio, Ops Tower, and safety boundaries into one disciplined ecosystem.

## 1. Planet OS Vision

Trading Pro Max is more than a workstation. It is a premium trading planet where each system has a defined purpose, state, owner, safety boundary, and truth rule.

The Planet OS exists to:

- keep the product coherent as it grows
- let Ahmad see and govern the whole ecosystem
- make citizen access and plan classes understandable
- separate safe automation from sensitive review
- prevent fake capability claims
- make future desktop, mobile, media, community, and monetization expansion orderly

The Planet OS must feel precise, premium, global, trustworthy, and operational. It must never become childish, decorative, or fantasy themed.

## 2. Founder King Command Room

Ahmad is the Founder King and Founder Commander. The Founder King Command Room is the future private owner-only desktop and mobile command surface.

It is:

- private owner-only
- not a public route
- not available to Free, Pro, VIP, or Enterprise citizens
- read-only by default
- protected by strong owner authentication
- audit-backed for every sensitive action
- designed for review, approval, and blocked-state visibility

It may eventually show:

- platform health
- citizens and account state
- plan distribution readiness
- feedback and hardening state
- Guardian incidents
- Legal review queue
- Media campaign queue
- AI/IQ summaries
- Engineering tasks
- Ops readiness
- beta and launch blockers
- revenue readiness later
- next recommended actions

It must never fake real users, revenue, followers, subscriptions, broker activation, feed activation, billing, monitoring, public launch, legal certification, or Islamic/Sharia certification.

## 5. Automation Levels

| Level | Meaning | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| auto | safe action can run without special review | low-risk guidance, non-sensitive summaries |
| review | human or operator review required | feedback triage, community moderation, claim review |
| founder approval | high-risk action requires Founder King confirmation | public launch wording, media campaigns, entitlement activation |
| blocked | action is not allowed until future gates exist or remains prohibited | live execution, real-money routing, secrets exposure, fake claims |

Automation is useful only when it preserves truth. Automation must not silently activate sensitive capabilities.

## 6. Safety Boundaries

The Planet OS safety boundary is:

- Low risk: auto allowed
- Medium risk: review required
- High risk: Founder approval required
- Critical risk: blocked

Critical and high-risk areas include:

- live execution
- real-money routing
- broker/feed activation
- billing and checkout
- media publishing
- Pro/VIP claims
- Islamic/Sharia wording
- public launch
- secret-related actions
- Founder Command Room sensitive actions

Every sensitive or blocked action should create an audit event when implemented.

# No Global Launch Without Brand Clearance

Public/global launch is blocked until the final public brand passes:

- WIPO Global Brand Database review
- USPTO review
- EUIPO / TMview review
- Swiss IPI / Swissreg review if Swiss-facing use is planned
- domain review for `.com`, `.ai`, `.app`, and `.ch`
- phonetic, visual, sector, language, and reputation review
- legal review
- Ahmad final approval

The app may generate manual tasks. It must not buy domains, file trademarks, make legal claims, or launch.

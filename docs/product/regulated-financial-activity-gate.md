# Regulated Financial Activity Gate

Financial services, customer funds, custody, brokerage, broker/feed activation,
live execution, real money, and financial advice require legal and regulatory
review before any real-world movement.

If applicable, FINMA/legal review, KYC/AML analysis, privacy review, security
review, operational readiness, support readiness, rollback readiness, and
Founder final approval are required.

In local/laptop scope these activities remain blocked or black-holed. Alkon may
only prepare internal readiness reports and safe public blocked-state wording.

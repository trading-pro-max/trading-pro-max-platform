# Local Day One Acceptance

Local Day One Acceptance is the final internal gate before Ahmad starts Trading Pro Max as a closed local product on his machine.

It answers one question only:

Can Trading Pro Max begin Local Operations Day 1 as a local, paper-safe, non-launch review product?

It does not evaluate global launch readiness.

## Categories

The gate reviews 18 categories:

1. runtime readiness
2. public entry readiness
3. Trading Workspace readiness
4. chart readiness
5. paper execution readiness
6. TPM Assistant readiness
7. Why Blocked readiness
8. Journal / Coach readiness
9. Settings readiness
10. Diagnostics readiness
11. plan language readiness
12. Product Truth readiness
13. Founder Command privacy readiness
14. local operations protocol readiness
15. product memory readiness
16. visual acceptance readiness
17. security/safety readiness
18. Git/validation readiness

Statuses:

- pass
- partial
- blocker
- planned
- blocked_by_design
- needs_ahmad_review

`needs_ahmad_review` is not a launch approval. It means the product is ready for Ahmad's local visual judgment.

## Local Day One Decision

Local Day One can start when there are no blockers and dangerous systems remain blocked or inactive.

Ahmad human visual review remains required for final acceptance. No system may self-certify a perfect visual result or global readiness.

## Hard Boundaries

Local Day One never activates:

- public launch
- production
- billing
- broker/feed
- live execution
- real-money routing
- social publishing
- paid plan entitlement
- fake users, revenue, or metrics

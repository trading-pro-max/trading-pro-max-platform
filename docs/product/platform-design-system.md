# Platform Design System

The Trading Pro Max platform design system keeps all surfaces recognizable as one product while adapting density and hierarchy to each platform.

## Core System

- Living Earth Mark for SVG identity.
- Swiss-inspired precision for spacing, typography, and motion restraint.
- Chart-first workstation hierarchy.
- Compact readiness labels tied to Product Truth.
- Public plan language: Free, Pro, VIP, Institutional.
- TPM Assistant as the public assistant name.

## Web Public

- small premium Earth Mark
- clean hero and direct route to the Trading Workspace
- simple plan truth
- no internal Founder/governance language
- no fake live, billing, broker/feed, launch, or paid activation claims

## Web Workstation

- symbol / price / status first
- chart primary
- execution rail strong and clear
- Assistant collapsed and non-blocking
- Journal / Coach secondary
- plan state quiet
- Why Blocked compact

## Desktop Future

Future desktop design may use a denser trading shell, but it is not shipped and must not be claimed as active. It must preserve chart dominance, paper-safe truth, and reduced-motion safety.

## Mobile Future

Future mobile design should prioritize review, learning, settings, readiness, and concise workspace context. It must not compress the full desktop terminal into a cluttered phone view.

## Founder Command Future

Founder Command desktop/mobile visuals may use stronger graphite/gold command styling and subtle Swiss red. They remain owner-only, internal, hidden from public navigation, and outside user plans.

## Design Quality Rules

- no childish or fantasy UI
- no copied competitor branding
- no over-glow
- no clutter
- no giant dead space
- no boxed/small workstation
- no chart obstruction
- no command language in public user surfaces

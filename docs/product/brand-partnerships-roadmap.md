# Brand Partnerships Roadmap

Brand partnerships are future business development concepts. No partnership is active, implied, contracted, or user-visible in the current product.

## Future Partnership Types

- Swiss precision partnership
- sponsored clock edition
- VIP experience partnership
- education/content sponsorship
- platform theme partnership
- event/webinar partnership
- academy partnership

## Required Conditions

- signed contract
- brand asset approval
- Rights/IP review
- Legal review
- Guardian review
- Founder approval
- product truth review

## Blocked Claims

- fake partnership
- implied endorsement
- competitor copying
- uncontracted brand asset use
- financial performance claims
- user manipulation through fake status or scarcity

Brand partnership work remains planned-only until a future explicit approval phase.

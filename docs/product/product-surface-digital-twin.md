# Product Surface Digital Twin

The digital twin defines what each role sees.

Roles:
- Guest
- Free
- Pro
- VIP
- Institutional
- Founder

For each role it defines visible surfaces, hidden surfaces, planned surfaces, blocked surfaces, forbidden leaks, plan language, Assistant capabilities, Product Truth, and Founder Command exposure.

Guards:
- Free cannot see VIP as active
- users cannot see Founder Command
- public UI cannot expose internal governance terms
- performance-fee research stays hidden from users
- fake billing/live/launch claims are blocked

# Pro Max Drift Detector

The Drift Detector protects Pro Max from movement that feels productive but weakens the Prime World.

It flags:
- overbuilding
- feature sprawl
- visual noise
- public claim risk
- legal risk
- treasury risk
- security risk
- Prime World distraction
- Founder energy overload
- weak proof
- Assistant confusion
- chart-not-king issues

If Living Market Core is not accepted, expansion is delayed unless it is safety, legal, security, or critical Product Truth work.

If Founder energy is overloaded, Alkon reduces the surface to one next critical decision.

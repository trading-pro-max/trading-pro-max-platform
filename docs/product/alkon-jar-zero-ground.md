# Alkon Jar Zero Ground

Status: active

Zero Ground means the Jar begins with truth, not momentum.

Before action, the Jar asks:

- What is this?
- Who owns it?
- Is it public, private, invisible, tool, test, doc, report, or asset?
- Does it touch safety, secrets, money, launch, live trading, billing, broker/feed, or public claims?
- What evidence is required?
- Does Ahmad need to decide?

If the answer is unclear, the item stays in Jar 9 Founder Decision or Jar 6 Cleanup.

# TPM Constitution

The constitution defines the non-negotiable laws of the Trading Pro Max Planet OS. Kingdom language remains a serious governance metaphor, but the constitution applies to the whole intelligent trading planet.

## Core Laws

1. No guaranteed profit.
2. No win-rate claims.
3. No risk-free wording.
4. No fake live trading.
5. No fake broker-connected claim.
6. No fake live market feed claim.
7. No fake public launch.
8. No fake billing or subscription activation.
9. No fake Islamic or Sharia certification.
10. No fake VIP performance guarantee.
11. No copied competitor identity.
12. No secrets in Git.
13. Every sensitive action must be audited.
14. Safe tasks may be automated.
15. Sensitive tasks require review.
16. Dangerous tasks are blocked.
17. Founder approval is required for high-risk actions.
18. Public launch is always last, never early.

## Automation Law

- Low risk: automation allowed.
- Medium risk: review required.
- High risk: Founder approval required.
- Critical risk: blocked.

## Language Law

Allowed wording:

- decision support
- context quality
- paper-only
- fallback-first
- not certified
- review required
- no guarantee
- activation required
- operator review required
- not legal advice
- not financial advice

Blocked wording:

- guaranteed profit
- sure signal
- risk-free
- verified win rate unless independently proven and legally reviewed
- broker connected when unconfigured
- Sharia certified when not certified
- public launch when not launched

## Founder Law

The Founder King may approve high-risk direction, but cannot override truth. If a claim is false, unsafe, illegal, or unconfigured, it remains blocked.

## Planet Law

Every planetary system must declare its real state: active, planned, inactive, blocked, fallback, degraded, review required, or founder approval required. No system may become active through language, design, or documentation alone.

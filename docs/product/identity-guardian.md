# Identity Guardian

Identity Guardian reviews brand expression before it reaches public or owner-only surfaces.

## Blocks

- visual clutter
- excessive motion
- casino/neon styling
- childish or fantasy visuals
- internal terms in public UI
- restricted command identity as a user plan
- legacy non-public plan labels
- fake Swiss legal/company claim
- fake Sharia certification
- fake Pro, VIP, or Institutional activation
- fake partnership
- uncontracted brand/company use
- motion that distracts from the chart
- occasion themes with political, religious, copyright, or partnership risk unless explicitly configured and reviewed

## Outcomes

- safe
- caution
- review_required
- founder_approval_required
- blocked

## Safe Alternative

Use public-safe Trading Pro Max language, low motion, no fake claims, no restricted internal terms, and no activation wording for live, billing, broker/feed, real money, social publishing, or public launch.

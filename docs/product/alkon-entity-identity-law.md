# Alkon Entity Identity Law

No anonymous entity may live inside Alkon.

Every entity receives an ID, name, category, world, owner, surface, visibility, risk, lifecycle state, and report target. Public Earth entities require public purpose. Private Alkon entities stay Founder-only. Invisible operating entities protect truth, safety, validation, memory, and boundaries.

Sensitive or cleanup entities must have an owner and dependency information before they can be accepted.

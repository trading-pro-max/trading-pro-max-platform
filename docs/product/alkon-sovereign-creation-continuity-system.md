The lifecycle is: birth, identity, law, function, integration, proof, life monitoring, evolution, deprecation, removal, memory, and Founder report.

This is not public product language. Trading Pro Max users remain in the Earth-native public world. Alkon remains private, read-only, non-executing, secret-free, and governed by Founder authority.

Continuity cannot launch production, activate billing, connect broker/feed, enable live execution, route real money, publish socially, run shell commands, call Codex, delete files, expose secrets, expose bank/card data, create accounts, send email, or generate raster assets.

# Al-Kawn Swiss Luxury Living Style

The Al-Kawn visual style is alive, premium, Swiss-precise, dark, calm, deep, clean, and serious. It is not generic SaaS, not toy neon, not noisy, and not decorative without meaning.

## Required Wording

- الكون حي داخل لابتوب أحمد.
- كل تفصيل داخل الكون له معنى.
- كل نبض يعكس حالة وليس زينة.
- The pulse reflects state, not decoration.
- الكون بفخامة سويسرية تليق باسمه.
- Swiss-inspired precision, not official Swiss endorsement.
- Product Truth remains visible above visual beauty.

## Tokens

Canonical token files:

- `app/_styles/al-kawn-swiss-luxury-tokens.css`
- `app/_styles/al-kawn-living-visual-tokens.css`

Tokens include cosmic black, graphite black, glacier white, moon silver, atmosphere blue, Earth deep blue, Swiss red precision, truth amber, danger red, protected blue/violet, glass panel background, precision border, soft shadow, subtle glow, focus ring, and text states.

## Motion

Motion must communicate state: wake pulse, protected pulse, report-ready marker, and blocked-state clarity. Reduced-motion fallback is required.

# Infinite Growth Under Swiss-Law Gravity

Swiss-law gravity measures how close a growth request is to regulated reality.
Local ideas, docs, tests, audits, memory, and local build are low gravity.
Public copy, Assistant behavior, and user-facing UI carry public-claim gravity.
User data carries privacy gravity. Money, billing, invoices, tax, and accounting
carry money gravity. Media, ads, and social content carry claims gravity.
Launch and production carry launch gravity. Financial services, broker/feed,
live execution, customer funds, and real money carry critical regulatory gravity.

Black-hole gravity applies when a local request tries to activate live trading,
real money, broker/feed, billing, production secrets, bank/card data, payment
execution, social publishing, fake claims, or public Alkon exposure.

Swiss-law gravity does not execute. It assigns review, delay, block, or safe
creation scope.

# Pro Max Living Earth Runtime

Pro Max Living Earth Runtime is the public identity system that makes Pro Max feel like an Earth-native digital world through code-driven rendering.

Earth is not decoration, wallpaper, or a marketing backdrop. Earth is the public soul of Pro Max: it connects Pro Max Center, the Pro Max logo, Pro Max Trading, Settings, Diagnostics, and future public worlds.

Current truth:
- Rendering mode: procedural / hybrid fallback.
- Asset status: no external images, no unknown-license assets, no generated images.
- Photorealism: not claimed without an approved local licensed texture.
- Trading rule: Earth may support atmosphere, but it must never cover market reading.
- Governance: Alkon reviews Earth evolution privately; public users never see internal governance.

Local Day One remains gated by Ahmad visual acceptance.

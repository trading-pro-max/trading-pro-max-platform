# Alkon Entity Life Monitoring

Accepted entities are monitored for usefulness, ownership, tests, Product Truth, clutter, duplication, leaks, Founder preference alignment, and public Earth value.

Life states include healthy, needs improvement, stale, duplicated, risky, cleanup candidate, and deprecation candidate.

Monitoring is read-only. It creates reports and next safe actions; it does not delete, publish, launch, bill, trade, or execute external actions.

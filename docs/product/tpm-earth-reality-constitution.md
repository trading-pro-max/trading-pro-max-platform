# TPM Earth Reality Constitution

Earth is the public reference reality for Trading Pro Max. It is not only a logo, background, theme, Free-plan visual, or decoration.

Every public visual, plan, Assistant response, workspace behavior, market surface, support path, environment state, legal/safety truth, and launch gate must serve a real human on Earth under truth, safety, law, time, place, privacy, and trust.

Core law:
- Earth is the reference reality.
- Public users live in Earth reality.
- Every public claim must be truthful.
- Every public flow must respect law, safety, privacy, time, place, learning, support, and trust.
- Private command systems protect Earth from behind the scenes and remain private.

Earth Reality checks:
- Human orientation and accessibility.
- Time and atmosphere truth.
- Place/privacy boundaries.
- Market and paper-safe truth.
- Law and trust.
- Learning and support.
- Environment and code-only identity.
- Product Truth and launch gates.

Blocked:
- Live execution activation.
- Real-money routing.
- Broker/feed activation.
- Billing activation.
- Production launch claims.
- Social publishing.
- Fake users, revenue, metrics, plan activation, legal/company status, Sharia certification, partnerships, support, or downloads.
- Public exposure of private internal systems.

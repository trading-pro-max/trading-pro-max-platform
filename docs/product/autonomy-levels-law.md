# Autonomy Levels Law

## Allowed Now

- Level 1 detect: classify and report.
- Level 2 draft: prepare review-only artifacts.
- Level 3.0 Codex-ready task draft only: create prompts, passports, and validation plans.
- Level 3.1 approved low-risk Codex submission readiness only: prepare manual/CLI/cloud/GitHub text, with no in-app execution.

## Not Allowed Now

- Level 0 blocked categories cannot receive valid implementation passports.
- Level 4 future low-risk auto-fix is not active.
- Level 5 is forbidden.

No autonomy level may bypass Product Truth, Guardian, Legal, Trust Governor, Security, Secrets, Visual Acceptance, or Founder approval gates.

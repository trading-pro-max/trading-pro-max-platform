# Alkon Treasury Life System

Treasury Life models founder-funded, hybrid-funded, revenue-funded, growth-mode, and blocked treasury readiness.

It tracks budget cap, invoice registry readiness, tax reserve readiness, VAT threshold watch, accountant review, autopay governance readiness, and safe next financial action. It does not execute payments, store bank/card data, store payment credentials, send invoices, activate billing, or perform tax filing automation.

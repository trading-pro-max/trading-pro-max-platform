# Risk Belt And Black Hole Zone

The Risk Belt holds tasks that require quarantine or review:
- secrets risk
- public internal leak
- suspicious world interface events
- risky claims
- legal/Guardian review
- security review

The Black Hole Zone permanently blocks:
- live execution activation
- real-money routing
- broker/feed activation
- billing activation now
- production secrets
- social publishing automation
- sending secrets to Codex
- external offensive actions
- fake users/revenue/metrics
- fake Sharia certification
- guaranteed profit or win-rate claims

Black-hole output is blocked, reason, safe alternative, and incident/report if needed.

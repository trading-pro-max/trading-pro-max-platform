## Why Cleanup Was Needed

Ahmad reviewed `/desktop/kawn` after the emergency correction. The route improved, but it still carried too many dashboard-like panels, technical fragments, Pro Max-heavy surfaces, and right-side queues close to the main experience.

## First Screen Rule

The first screen must feel like entering الكون. It has four primary areas:

- Living Entry.
- Human message from الكون.
- One Next Action.
- Boundary Strip.

The required visible truth remains:

- `الكون حي داخل لابتوب أحمد.`
- `هذا ليس Dashboard؛ هذا بيت الكون الحي.`

- `أحمد وحده يتحكم بحركة المال الحقيقي.`
- `Local Day One لم يبدأ بعد.`

## Pro Max Demotion Rule

الكون is root. Pro Max Galaxy is a future layer inside الكون. `/trading` remains available as the Earth Planet trading surface, but it must not dominate `/desktop/kawn`.

## Technical Panels Secondary Rule

Technical systems are grouped below the living entry:

- System Details.
- Boot Details.
- Product Truth Details.
- Infinity / Operator Details.
- Reports.
- Control Surfaces.
- Rights / Ownership.
- Packaging / Auth Gates.

## Right-Side Queue Cleanup Rule

Queues are secondary to one next action. Decision, task, appointment, blocker, legal/money, and report queues may remain available, but they must not look like the main purpose of الكون.

## Local Day One Review Rule

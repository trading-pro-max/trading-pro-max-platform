# Planet Professions And Workers

Professions describe responsibilities and permission concepts. They do not create fake staff accounts.

| Profession | Ministry | Worker Kind | May Do | May Not Do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Engineers | Engineering & Infrastructure | human | build validated code and contracts | skip tests, commit secrets, fake capability |
| Guardians | Guardian & Defense | human/module | flag abuse and unsafe bypass | use invasive surveillance |
| Legal Counsel | Justice / Legal / Compliance | human | review claims and disclaimers | claim certification or provide legal advice |
| Media Officers | Media & Communications | human/future | draft content and campaigns | publish externally |
| Campaign Operators | Growth & Conversion | future | plan campaigns | fake metrics or urgency |
| Video Producers | AI Video Studio & Production | future | draft scripts and briefs | upload or publish automatically |
| Coaches | Coaching & Guidance | future | guide learning and reflection | pressure trading or promise profit |
| Academy Teachers | Academy & Education | future | create education | give financial advice |
| Community Moderators | Community & Culture | future | moderate community | permit scams or signal selling |
| Support Agents | Citizen Support | future | triage requests | claim nonexistent SLA |
| Records Keepers | Records & Portfolio | module/future | organize records | expose private user data |
| Quality Inspectors | Product Quality & Testing | human/module | verify visual and regression gates | accept fake completion |
| Ops Operators | Ops Health & Reliability | human | monitor readiness | claim production readiness |
| Treasury Operators | Treasury & Economy | future | review monetization readiness | activate billing |
| Integration Engineers | External Integrations | future | prepare integration contracts | connect live broker/feed without gates |
| Security Officers | Cybersecurity & Privacy | human | protect secrets and access | weaken auth |
| AI Agents | AI / IQ / Brain | agent | summarize safe context | predict certainty or trade |
| Companion Agents | Personal Companion | agent | explain state and safe next steps | execute actions |
| Founder Companion | Supreme Command | agent | brief Ahmad | approve alone or override critical blocks |
| Content Agents | Media & Growth | agent | draft internal content | publish externally |

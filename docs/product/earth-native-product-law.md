# Earth-Native Product Law

Trading Pro Max public surfaces must feel useful to a real person on Earth before they feel impressive as a system.

The product must answer:
- Where am I?
- What can I do here?
- What is active now?
- What is planned or future?
- What is blocked or inactive?
- What is the next safe step?

Earth-native does not mean simple decoration. It means the product is anchored in human orientation, time, place, privacy, market truth, law, learning, support, environment, and Product Truth.

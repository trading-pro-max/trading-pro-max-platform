# Entity Deprecation And Removal Law

An entity may be deprecated when it is duplicate, unclear, replaced by a stronger entity, risky, cluttering, ownerless, unvalidated, low value with high maintenance, or repeatedly rejected in visual review.

An entity may be removed only when:
- dependents are migrated
- Product Truth is preserved
- tests are updated
- public/private boundaries are preserved
- rollback is safe
- a report is generated

Never auto-delete security systems, Secrets Authority, Product Truth, plan entitlements, public/private boundaries, launch gates, Alkon core, or Product Memory without Founder approval.

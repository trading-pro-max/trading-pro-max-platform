# Local Product Reality Score

The Local Product Reality Score is a 0-10 internal review score for closed Local Day One readiness.

It is not a launch score and must not be presented as public market readiness.

## Score Areas

- public clarity
- workstation clarity
- chart dominance
- execution clarity
- Assistant usefulness
- Journal / Coach usefulness
- plan clarity
- Settings organization
- Diagnostics organization
- visual maturity
- Swiss identity
- Earth Mark identity
- Product Truth
- Safety / Legal
- Founder Command privacy
- local operations readiness
- user simplicity
- internal integration

Every score includes a reason, next action, and whether Ahmad human acceptance is still needed.

## Rules

- no fake 10/10
- Ahmad visual acceptance is required
- global launch readiness is not claimed
- fake users, revenue, and metrics are forbidden
- Product Truth remains the source of truth for blocked systems

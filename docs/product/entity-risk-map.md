# Entity Risk Map

Risk dimensions:
- public leak risk
- secret risk
- fake claim risk
- auth/security risk
- visual clutter risk
- Product Truth risk
- launch activation risk
- maintenance complexity

Risk levels:
- safe
- review required
- Founder approval required
- blocked
- black hole

Black-hole risk includes live execution, real money, broker/feed activation, billing now, production secrets, public launch activation, social publishing, fake claims, and Alkon public exposure.

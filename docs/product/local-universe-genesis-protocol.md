# Local Universe Genesis Protocol

Trading Pro Max must live first as a closed local product on Ahmad's machine before any global activation.

Doctrine:
- Ahmad's device is the temporary local capital.
- Trading Pro Max operates first as a local closed universe.
- Local operation exists for testing, observation, refinement, and Founder acceptance.
- Global launch is forbidden until explicitly approved in a later phase.
- Local product truth must stay visible and honest.

Hard boundaries:
- local only
- paper-safe only
- non-launch
- non-production
- no billing
- no broker/feed activation
- no real-money routing
- no social publishing
- no fake users, revenue, or metrics

The Local Universe can produce reports, checklists, acceptance notes, and safe build tasks. It must not activate live systems.

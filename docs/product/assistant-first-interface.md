# Assistant-First Interface

The Assistant-first interface keeps essential controls visible and moves secondary or complex control into TPM Assistant.

Visible controls stay for:
- public navigation
- sign in
- entering the Trading Workspace
- paper buy/sell rehearsal
- AI wait
- Why Blocked when relevant
- Settings and Diagnostics

TPM Assistant handles:
- calmer workspace
- bigger chart
- low motion, Static Mode, High Contrast
- plan questions
- apps and platform truth
- support paths
- Journal and Coach prompts
- locked, planned, inactive, and future explanations

This reduces button clutter without hiding the actions a user needs immediately.

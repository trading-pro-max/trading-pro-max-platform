# Reality Passport Law

A Reality Passport is the private bridge between a valuable idea and a Command Passport.

It must include:
- purpose
- owner
- source
- public/private layer
- risk
- Jar classification
- allowed tools
- forbidden tools
- evidence required
- first step
- Ahmad decision requirement
- next fate
- Product Truth rules

A Reality Passport is preview-only. It cannot execute, launch, pay, trade, publish, delete, reveal secrets, or bypass Ahmad.

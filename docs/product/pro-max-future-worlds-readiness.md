# Pro Max Future Worlds Readiness

Future Pro Max Worlds are private readiness candidates only.

No future world receives public pages, launch copy, navigation, billing,
production activation, social publishing, user-data collection, or product
claims in this pass.

Every future world must pass Genesis gates: meaning, human need, market, law,
treasury, security, prototype, proof, Prime World protection, Founder approval,
and World Birth Permit.

Any future world that weakens Pro Max Trading is delayed or rejected.

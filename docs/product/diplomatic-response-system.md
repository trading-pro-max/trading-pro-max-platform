# Diplomatic Response System

## Purpose

The Diplomatic Response System prepares safe draft replies for future external communication.

## Draft Types

- Auto acknowledgement draft
- Support draft
- Partnership draft
- Media draft
- Legal review required
- Founder approval required
- Blocked reply

## Rules

- Drafts are not sent automatically.
- Sensitive replies require Legal, Guardian, Security, and Founder review as appropriate.
- Partnership and media replies require Founder approval.
- Legal notices require Legal review.
- Security alerts require Security and Guardian review.
- Secret requests, threats, impersonation, fake partnerships, scams, and phishing are quarantined.

## Product Truth

The system does not send emails, send DMs, publish posts, activate social accounts, or automate outreach.

# Al-Kawn Desktop Operating Environment Standard

## Definition

Al-Kawn Desktop Operating Environment is the private desktop command client for الكون.

It is not a public product, not a normal dashboard, not a generic chat app, and not a public web wrapper. It is Ahmad's private operating environment for seeing, commanding, and reviewing الكون on his own computer.

Required meaning:

- الكون هو نسخة أحمد الإلكترونية الخاصة.
- Al-Kawn Desktop is Ahmad's private operating environment.
- Desktop is the main private command client for الكون.
- Pro Max is the future public product, not الكون.
- Product Truth overrides every action.
- Universe Operating Kernel is the execution judge.
- داخل الكون: التنفيذ مباشر.
- عند القانون: يتوقف لأحمد.
- عند المال: يتوقف لأحمد.

## Private Ahmad-Only Rule

The desktop route is private in meaning and must not be exposed as a public product surface. Public desktop distribution is blocked until Ahmad explicitly approves distribution, security, signing, packaging, and legal gates.

No secrets may be stored in Git or inside a desktop app bundle. External account connections require Ahmad approval.

The desktop environment must show a real status sequence:

1. Ahmad Private Device context

Pending states must be labeled honestly. Native desktop shell packaging remains a future gate when no Electron/Tauri shell exists.

## Chat and Command Model

The center command area represents Ahmad speaking to الكون and الكون responding with Product Truth preserved.

Safe commands may organize internal work, reports, tasks, audits, and next-action planning. Legal, money, broker, public launch, brand, domain, secrets, and external account actions stop for Ahmad.

## Decision Center

Desktop decisions are classified as:

- Direct internal execution
- Legal stop
- Money stop
- Blocked by Product Truth

No official, legal, money, payment, broker, public launch, brand, domain, or irreversible action may be executed without Ahmad.

## Task Center

The task center may organize non-sensitive internal tasks. It must not store personal sensitive tasks in Git. Tasks must show layer, priority, status, category, next step, who decides, and reason.

## Appointment Center

Appointments are private and local-first. External calendar connection requires Ahmad approval. Sensitive appointments must not be committed to Git.

## Report Center

The report center shows closure reports, inventory reports, registry reports, cleanup reports, kernel reports, visual map reports, Product Truth reports, next safe action, and validation status. Missing reports must be shown honestly.

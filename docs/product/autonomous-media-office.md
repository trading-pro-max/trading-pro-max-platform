# Autonomous Media Office

The Autonomous Media Office plans and reviews content for Trading Pro Max. It does not connect social accounts, store tokens, publish externally, or generate real follower metrics.

## Mission

- grow the kingdom through safe education and product communication
- preserve brand voice
- avoid risky claims
- route sensitive content through Guardian, Legal Counsel, and Founder approval

## Content Types

- education posts
- academy lessons
- product tips
- paper-mode explanations
- fallback and blocked state explanations
- feature walkthroughs
- changelog summaries
- Pro/VIP teasers when reviewed
- community highlights when community exists

## Channels

- X / Twitter
- Instagram
- TikTok
- YouTube
- Facebook
- LinkedIn
- Telegram
- Discord
- Reddit
- Blog / Newsroom
- regional channels later

## Rules

- no account tokens in Git
- no external posting in this architecture phase
- no fake followers, impressions, revenue, or campaign success
- no copied competitor content
- no guaranteed profit, win-rate, sure signal, or risk-free claims
- no live, broker, feed, billing, public launch, or Islamic certification claim unless true

## Review Partners

- Brand Voice Editors check tone.
- Guardian checks abuse and manipulation risks.
- Legal Counsel checks claims.
- Founder King approves high-risk or public release content.

## Planet Earth Media Boundary

The Media & Communications Continent includes Media Office, AI Video Studio, Content Calendar, Campaigns, and Social Account Registry. No account connection, token storage, external publishing, fake followers, fake campaign metrics, or public launch claims are introduced by this model.

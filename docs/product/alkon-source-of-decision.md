# Alkon Source Of Decision

The source of decision is the private reason an action deserves attention.

A valid source must answer:

- What Founder vision does this serve?
- What real human or Founder value does it create?
- What truth does it preserve?
- What safety boundary does it protect?
- What proof will close it?
- Why is this the one correct action now?

Invalid sources include excitement, complexity, public hype, theory without
product impact, new-world expansion before Prime World acceptance, or work that
delays Living Market Core and Local Day One.

Source Law reduces drift by forcing every decision back to Pro Max Trading,
human usefulness, Product Truth, and evidence.

# AI Video Studio

The AI Video Studio is a future content planning workspace. It supports video ideas and scripts but does not integrate generation APIs, upload media, connect social accounts, or publish externally.

## Supported Planning Tasks

- video idea generation
- script generation
- platform-specific adaptation
- captions
- hashtags
- thumbnail brief
- voiceover brief
- scene outline
- compliance risk scoring
- publish readiness state

## Content Categories

- Academy videos
- product tutorials
- trust and safety videos
- Pro/VIP teasers when reviewed
- feature walkthroughs
- changelog summaries
- paper-mode explanations
- fallback and blocked-state explainers

## Review Requirements

Every video should include:

- brand review state
- Guardian review state
- Legal review state
- Founder approval state when high risk
- publish readiness state

## Blocked Content

- guaranteed profit
- win-rate claims
- risk-free trading
- fake live trading
- fake broker/feed/billing
- fake public launch
- fake Islamic/Sharia certification
- misleading urgency
- copied competitor visuals or scripts

## Current State

AI Video Studio is architecture only. No real generation, upload, or publishing automation exists.

## Planet Earth Studio Boundary

AI Video Studio belongs to the Media & Communications Continent. It may prepare ideas, scripts, captions, hashtags, thumbnail briefs, voiceover briefs, scene outlines, and compliance risk scores. It does not connect APIs, upload media, publish externally, or claim metrics.

Content Factory rules classify AI video scripts as approval_required unless they contain blocked claims, in which case they must be rewritten before review.

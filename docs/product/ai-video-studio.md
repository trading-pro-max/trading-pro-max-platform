# AI Video Studio

The AI Video Studio is a future content planning workspace. It supports video ideas and scripts but does not integrate generation APIs, upload media, connect social accounts, or publish externally.

## Supported Planning Tasks

- video idea generation
- script generation
- platform-specific adaptation
- captions
- hashtags
- thumbnail brief
- voiceover brief
- scene outline
- compliance risk scoring
- publish readiness state

## Content Categories

- Academy videos
- product tutorials
- trust and safety videos
- Pro/VIP teasers when reviewed
- feature walkthroughs
- changelog summaries
- paper-mode explanations
- fallback and blocked-state explainers

## Review Requirements

Every video should include:

- brand review state
- Guardian review state
- Legal review state
- Founder approval state when high risk
- publish readiness state

## Blocked Content

- guaranteed profit
- win-rate claims
- risk-free trading
- fake live trading
- fake broker/feed/billing
- fake public launch
- fake Islamic/Sharia certification
- misleading urgency
- copied competitor visuals or scripts

## Current State

AI Video Studio is architecture only. No real generation, upload, or publishing automation exists.

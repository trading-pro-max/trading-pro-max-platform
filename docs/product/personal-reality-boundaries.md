# Personal Reality Boundaries

Personal Reality can change comfort, readability, atmosphere, learning depth, Assistant style, and workspace layout.

It cannot:
- Activate billing.
- Activate paid plans.
- Enable live execution.
- Route real money.
- Connect broker/feed.
- Publish socially.
- Launch production.
- Reveal private systems.
- Weaken accessibility or safety.
- Turn weather, time, or sessions into trading advice.

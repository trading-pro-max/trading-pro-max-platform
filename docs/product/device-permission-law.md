# Device Permission Law

Each device sees only what matches its role.

Public Web:

- Public view.
- Account use when signed in.
- Paper workspace only.

Public Desktop:

- Planned paper workspace only.
- No installer or download claim.

Public Mobile:

- Planned companion only.
- No app store, push, APK, or IPA claim.

Public Tablet:

- Future learning and review only.

Alkon Desktop:

- Founder command readiness.
- Full internal reporting.
- No direct dangerous execution.

Alkon Mobile:

- Private status review.
- Idea capture readiness.
- Low-risk approval readiness later.
- No secrets and no execution.

Alkon Tablet:

- Visual review and reports only.

Alkon Watch:

- P0 alert-only.
- No secrets and no action execution.

Hard blocked on all devices: live execution activation, real money, billing activation, broker/feed activation, production secrets, social publishing, shell execution, public Alkon exposure, fake downloads, fake store claims, fake users, fake revenue, and fake metrics.

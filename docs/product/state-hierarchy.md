# Planet State Hierarchy

The TPM Planet OS state hierarchy defines authority, visibility, and safety across citizens, plans, workers, planetary systems, and Founder controls. Kingdom terms remain the governance metaphor inside the broader planet architecture.

## Hierarchy

1. Founder King / Founder Commander
2. Executive Council
3. Ministries
4. Independent Authorities
5. Workers / Employees
6. Citizens / Users
7. Plan-based classes
8. Product services
9. Safety and law boundaries

## State Layers

- Identity state: who is acting.
- Permission state: what they may access.
- Plan state: Free, Pro, VIP, Enterprise, or unavailable.
- Product state: active, planned, inactive, blocked, degraded, fallback.
- Safety state: low, medium, high, critical.
- Legal state: safe wording, review required, blocked claim.
- Audit state: event required, event recorded, event unavailable.

## Authority Rules

- Founder King is final approval authority for high-risk and public-facing changes.
- Legal Counsel can block claims and require review.
- Guardian can block suspicious, abusive, or unsafe behavior.
- Engineering can ship code only through validation and regression discipline.
- Citizens cannot access Founder Command Room or hidden operational controls.

## No Fake State

State must not imply a capability that is not genuinely configured. Planned, inactive, blocked, fallback, degraded, and review-required are valid planet states.

## Planet Earth Hierarchy

The current hierarchy expands into:

1. Founder King / Sovereign Creator
2. Founder Command Capital
3. Royal Executive Council
4. Continents
5. States
6. Ministries
7. Cities / Product Modules
8. Workers / Employees / AI Agents
9. Citizens / Users
10. Plan-based citizenship classes
11. Laws / Constitution / Safety Boundaries

This hierarchy organizes responsibility. It does not create public political authority, fake staff accounts, or hidden user privileges.

# Kingdom Staff Model

The staff model defines how real people, future internal tools, and AI-assisted workflows may support the kingdom without creating fake accounts or fake operational capacity.

## Staff Principles

- Staff are roles until real accounts and permissions exist.
- No fake employee profiles or fake support capacity.
- Every staff role has a permission concept before a UI surface.
- Sensitive roles require audit and review.
- AI may assist staff but must not silently approve high-risk actions.

## Permission Concepts

- Observe: view assigned readiness or queue data.
- Draft: prepare content, recommendations, tickets, or summaries.
- Review: mark items as reviewed or needs-work.
- Approve: limited future authority for low/medium items.
- Escalate: send high-risk items to Founder.
- Block: stop critical or unsafe items.

## Staff To Command Room

The Founder Command Room eventually receives staff summaries:

- Engineering: tests, bugs, technical debt, release blockers.
- Guardian: incidents, abuse score, false-positive notes.
- Legal: risky claims, policy changes, media review.
- Media: content calendar, risk classification, approval state.
- Treasury: plan readiness, entitlement gaps, billing blockers.
- Academy/Coach: content readiness and safety notes.
- Community: moderation load and risk patterns.

## What Staff May Not Do

- enable live execution
- route real money
- activate broker/feed/billing
- publish media externally
- claim public launch
- claim legal, Swiss, or Sharia certification
- expose secrets
- bypass Founder approval for high-risk actions

## Planet Earth Staff Model

Staff roles become professions inside TPM Planet Earth OS. Ministries may report profession summaries to the Founder Command Room, but those reports must remain readiness/truth summaries and must not invent workers, users, revenue, followers, or shipped apps.

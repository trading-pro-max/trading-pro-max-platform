# Localized Earth Focus System

Localized Earth Focus is broad-region only.

Allowed focus regions:

- europe
- middle_east
- north_america
- south_america
- east_asia
- africa
- oceania
- global

Inputs:

- user-selected broad region
- locale
- timezone

Forbidden:

- GPS
- exact city
- precise coordinates
- hidden tracking
- persistence of precise location
- external location calls

Public surfaces may show a broad focus label such as `Europe focus`. Founder/internal surfaces may inspect the focus basis, but no precise location is collected.
# Plan-Realm Focus Addendum

Localized Earth focus stays broad-region only and is separate from plan-realm atmosphere.

Realm atmosphere may change perspective and shape, but it must not increase location precision:

- Free Earth: broad Earth-native focus.
- Pro Orbit: broad orbital focus.
- VIP Lunar: broad lunar/deep-orbit focus.
- Institutional Station: broad station/control focus.
- Alkon Universe: private command focus only.

No precise location tracking, exact city targeting, external maps, raster assets, or public internal terminology are allowed.

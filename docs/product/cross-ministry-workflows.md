9. Journal/Coach Feature Workflow: Coach / Journal -> Presidency -> Legal -> Guardian -> AI Brain -> Quality.
10. Plan Entitlement Change Workflow: Treasury / Plans -> Presidency -> Legal -> Guardian -> Engineering -> Founder Approval.

## Template Fields

Each workflow defines steps, required reviews, risk level, automation level, blocked conditions, Founder decision point, product truth protections, and what must not be faked.

# Alkon Genesis Gates

Genesis gates decide whether a seed may continue.

- Meaning Gate: why the world should exist.
- Human Need Gate: real human, Founder, business, learning, support, or
  operational need.
- Market Gate: plausible audience without fake market claims.
- Law Gate: privacy, claims, financial advice, Swiss-law, and regulatory review.
- Treasury Gate: cost, burn, tax reserve, VAT watch, and no payment execution.
- Security Gate: secrets, auth, device trust, public/private boundary, and audit.
- Prototype Gate: docs, mock UI, and read-only readiness only.
- Proof Gate: no birth without proof.
- Prime World Protection Gate: Pro Max Trading cannot be weakened.
- Founder Gate: Ahmad final authority.
- Birth Gate: no world birth without all gates.

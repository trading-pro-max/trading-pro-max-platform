# Local Operations Doctrine

Trading Pro Max local operation is a closed, controlled review cycle.

Operating principles:
- The local machine is the first safe operating environment.
- Product truth outranks ambition.
- Founder acceptance is required before claiming visual or product maturity.
- The Free product path must remain familiar, professional, and paper-safe.
- Pro and VIP differentiation may be planned but not faked.
- Institutional remains future.
- Internal systems may guide the build, but public users must not see internal governance language.

Forbidden local shortcuts:
- claiming launch readiness without external gates
- enabling billing or checkout
- enabling real-money trading
- connecting broker/feed providers
- publishing to social media
- changing production secrets
- recording fake users, revenue, or metrics

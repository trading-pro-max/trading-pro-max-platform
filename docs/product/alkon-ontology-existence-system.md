# Alkon Ontology & Existence System

Alkon Ontology is the private existence layer of Trading Pro Max. It answers why every page, route, API, component, feature, plan realm, assistant intent, worker, memory lesson, test, risk, and launch gate exists.

Core law:
- Nothing exists inside Alkon without meaning.
- Nothing exists without an owner.
- Nothing exists without boundaries.
- Nothing exists without lifecycle.
- Nothing exists without relationships.
- Nothing exists without risk classification.
- Nothing exists without validation.
- Nothing exists without report target.
- Nothing exists without memory rule.
- Nothing remains forever without a reason to stay.

This is local, private, read-only readiness. It does not launch, bill, trade live, connect brokers, send email, publish, execute shell commands, call Codex, expose secrets, or expose Alkon publicly.

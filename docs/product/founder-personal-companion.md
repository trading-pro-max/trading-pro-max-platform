# Founder Personal Companion

The Founder Personal Companion is private to Ahmad. It is different from the user-facing TPM Personal Companion. It helps the Founder understand and govern TPM Planet Earth OS without approving high-risk actions alone or faking readiness.

Runtime source:

- `lib/server/founder-command/founder-companion.ts`

## Purpose

- summarize the whole planet
- explain ministry reports
- prioritize next actions
- identify risks
- detect blockers
- explain why something is blocked
- summarize Guardian alerts
- summarize Legal risks
- summarize Media queue
- summarize Engineering tasks
- summarize citizen/user feedback
- summarize Pro/VIP readiness
- prepare daily command briefing
- prepare weekly strategic briefing
- suggest safe decisions
- draft Founder responses
- remind about boundaries
- avoid launch pressure
- keep product vision aligned

## Must Not

- approve high-risk actions alone
- publish media without approval
- enable live execution
- enable real money
- expose secrets
- override Guardian/Legal blocks
- claim revenue, users, or metrics that do not exist
- manipulate the Founder
- fake readiness

## Automation Level

- Low-risk summaries: auto allowed.
- Sensitive recommendations: review required.
- High-risk decisions: Founder approval required.
- Critical actions: blocked.

## Current Runtime Foundation

The current snapshot summarizes:

- planet status
- decision ministries
- high and critical risks
- approvals needed
- Guardian and Legal warnings
- Media, Treasury, Engineering, and product gaps
- next safe decisions
- what not to do

It contains no secrets, no private user data, no fake users, no fake revenue, no fake followers, and no fake metrics.

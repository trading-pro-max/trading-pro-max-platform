# TPM Economy Roadmap

The TPM economy describes how plans, entitlements, support levels, reports, and future billing can work. It is a roadmap, not an active commercial system.

## Treasury Ministry Ownership

The Treasury Ministry owns:

- Free / Demo, Pro, VIP, and Enterprise plan definitions
- entitlement readiness
- billing readiness
- checkout truth
- customer lifecycle readiness
- support path readiness
- commercial launch blockers

## Economy Phases

1. Paper learning economy: free safe use, feedback, education, and trust building.
2. Pro readiness: entitlement model, plan copy, support path, Legal review.
3. VIP readiness: premium assistant, reports, private rooms, priority support, anti-claim controls.
4. Enterprise readiness: teams, admin, audit, runbooks, custom support.
5. Billing activation: only after provider configuration, policy review, support readiness, and Founder approval.

## No Fake Revenue

The platform must not claim:

- active subscriptions
- real revenue
- paying users
- checkout availability
- billing provider activation
- Pro, VIP, or Enterprise access

unless those systems are genuinely configured and released.

## Monetization Safety

Every paid claim requires:

- clear plan capability truth
- refund and cancellation support
- Legal Counsel review
- Guardian abuse review
- Founder approval for public launch language

Billing is never a shortcut around execution, broker, feed, or risk controls.

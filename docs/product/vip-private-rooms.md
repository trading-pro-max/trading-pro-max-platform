# VIP Private Rooms

VIP Private Rooms are future premium community and support spaces. They are not active today and must not be presented as paid access until entitlement, billing, moderation, and Legal review exist.

## Future Room Types

- VIP strategy rooms
- advanced coaching rooms
- performance discussion rooms
- priority support rooms
- private workspace sharing rooms
- premium report review rooms

## Governance

VIP rooms require:

- Guardian moderation
- Legal claim rules
- anti-scam enforcement
- no signal-selling behavior
- no guaranteed results
- no fake profit screenshots
- no copy trading unless a future compliant product explicitly supports it

## Safe VIP Language

- advanced context
- deeper review
- priority support readiness
- premium learning
- no guarantee

## Blocked VIP Language

- guaranteed signals
- VIP profit path
- risk-free strategy
- Sharia certified unless real certification exists
- live-money access by VIP status alone

## Current State

VIP Private Rooms are roadmap architecture only.

# Earth Human Layer

The Earth Human Layer checks that public Trading Pro Max serves people, not metrics.

Requirements:
- Home explains what the product is.
- Users can find Trading Workspace, Markets, Plans, Apps / Platforms, Academy, Community, Support, Settings, and Diagnostics.
- Users understand active, planned, future, inactive, and blocked states.
- Free feels complete, not cheap.
- Accessibility includes Static Mode, Low Motion, and High Contrast.
- Upgrade pressure stays calm and truthful.

# Pro Max Founder Energy Gate

Founder energy is a protected resource.

The Founder Energy Gate asks:
- Does Ahmad need to decide this now?
- Can Codex draft it without authority?
- Can it wait?
- Is it visual, legal, money, security, launch, or future-world sensitive?
- Is this the one decision that matters today?

Rules:
- avoid presenting many decisions at once
- summarize to one next critical decision
- delegate low-risk drafting
- request Founder decision for sensitive matters
- delay nonessential expansion

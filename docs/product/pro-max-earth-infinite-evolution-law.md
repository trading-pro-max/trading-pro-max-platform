# Pro Max Earth Infinite Evolution Law

The Pro Max Earth may improve forever, but every improvement must pass the gates before it appears in public.

Required gates:
- Asset Law.
- Product Truth.
- Visual Acceptance.
- Performance sanity.
- Accessibility.
- Public/private boundary.
- Evidence screenshots.
- Ahmad final acceptance for visual-sensitive changes.

Blocked actions:
- external image hotlink
- unknown-license texture
- generated Earth image
- fake Swiss regulatory claim
- Earth overlay covering the Trading chart
- Local Day One start without Ahmad

Every Earth improvement returns to evidence and Zero Truth.

# Pro Max Working Name Law

Status: active

Pro Max is a working name.

It is not:

- a cleared final global brand
- a registered trademark claim
- a globally exclusive ownership claim
- public launch readiness

Why:

- Pro Max uses generic words.
- Pro Max has heavy market use.
- Pro Max has likely conflict risk.
- Dell Pro Max creates conflict risk.
- Apple Pro Max creates association risk.
- Exclusive ownership potential is weak until reviewed.

Allowed:

- Continue development under Pro Max as the current working public name.
- Keep Pro Max public copy truthful and non-exclusive.

Blocked:

- Do not claim Ahmad owns Pro Max globally.
- Do not claim Pro Max is trademark-cleared.
- Do not launch globally under Pro Max as final brand.

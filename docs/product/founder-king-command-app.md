# Founder King Command App

The Founder King Command App is the future private owner-only command application for Ahmad. It is the device-level home of the Founder King Command Room inside the Trading Pro Max Planet OS.

This foundation is architecture, documentation, and contract readiness only. It does not ship native desktop or mobile apps, does not expose a public route, and does not activate production, broker, feed, billing, social media, or launch systems.

## What It Is

- a private command application for the Founder King
- separate from normal Trading Pro Max citizen surfaces
- designed for desktop and mobile owner devices
- read-only by default
- approval-oriented for sensitive reviewed actions
- audit-backed for every sensitive action
- serious, Swiss-inspired, and operational

## Why It Is Private

The Founder Command App can eventually see sensitive readiness state, risk state, approval queues, and operational blockers. That information is not appropriate for Free, Pro, VIP, Enterprise, public visitors, search engines, or general admin-style routes.

The app must remain private because it may eventually coordinate:

- platform health
- Guardian alerts
- Legal Counsel review queue
- Media Office campaigns
- AI Video Studio drafts
- TPM Brain summaries
- Companion activity
- feedback and hardening loop
- Community readiness
- Academy, Journal, and Coach systems
- Treasury and monetization readiness
- Pro, VIP, and Enterprise plan readiness
- Engineering tasks
- Product gaps
- Safety boundaries
- launch blockers
- secret rotation readiness
- production blockers
- Founder approval queue

## What It Can View Later

- active, planned, inactive, blocked, and degraded Planet OS systems
- queue summaries without exposing secrets
- readiness blockers
- risk classifications
- Guardian and Legal review status
- Media and AI Video draft state
- Treasury readiness, without billing activation
- Engineering and product quality state
- operational recovery notes

## What It Can Approve Later

Only future reviewed and allowed actions may be approved:

- media drafts after Guardian and Legal review
- AI video scripts after review
- campaign language after review
- Pro/VIP wording after review
- Islamic account wording only when truthful and reviewed
- launch wording only when all gates are satisfied
- sensitive platform changes only when explicitly configured

Founder approval never bypasses Guardian hard blocks, Legal hard blocks, configured safety gates, auth requirements, or truth requirements.

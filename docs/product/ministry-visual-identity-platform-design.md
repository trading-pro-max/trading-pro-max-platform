# Ministry of Visual Identity, Plan Experience & Platform Design

This internal ministry owns the visual identity and platform design system for Trading Pro Max. It is an internal Founder/operations structure only; normal users must not see ministry terminology in public UI.

## Mission

Ensure every visible surface feels like Trading Pro Max:

- Free feels familiar, clean, premium, and paper-safe.
- Pro feels professional, intelligent, and focused.
- VIP feels elite, deep, premium, and differentiated.
- Institutional feels formal, controlled, team-ready, and future.
- Founder Command remains private, sovereign, command-style, and internal only.
- The chart/workstation, repaired Earth Mark, and public language rules stay protected.

| Layer | Identity | State |

| Pro | graphite / emerald / silver; professional and focused | planned/locked unless entitlement exists |

Founder Command is not a user plan, not a paid upgrade, and not public navigation.

## Platform Experience Mandate

- **Web public:** simple, premium, public-safe, small Earth Mark, clear plan truth.
- **Web workstation:** chart-first, execution-clear, Assistant compact, no visual clutter.
- **Desktop future:** future shell only; no shipped desktop app claim.
- **Mobile future:** future simplified review/learning/settings/readiness surface; no shipped mobile app claim.
- **Founder Command desktop/mobile future:** owner-only command density and briefing; no public exposure or approval execution.

## Integration Contract

This ministry connects with:

- Visual Acceptance Engine for score estimates and human Ahmad review requirement.
- Product Truth for blocked/inactive/planned capability truth.
- Plan Entitlements for Free / Pro / VIP / Institutional activation boundaries.
- Founder Command readiness for private internal command visuals.
- Product Memory for known visual gaps, founder preferences, and validation history.

## Forbidden Scope

The ministry cannot enable launch, production, live execution, real money, broker/feed activation, billing, social publishing, public Founder Command exposure, fake paid plan activation, fake Swiss legal/company status, fake Islamic/Sharia certification, or fake users/revenue/metrics.

# AI Video Studio Workflow

AI Video Studio is a script and review readiness layer. It helps prepare future educational video concepts without generating, uploading, or publishing video.

## Supported Artifacts

- idea
- short script
- long script
- captions
- hashtags
- thumbnail brief
- voiceover brief
- scene outline
- compliance risk score

## Review Lifecycle

AI video artifacts follow the same content lifecycle:

idea -> draft -> brand review -> Guardian review -> Legal review -> Founder approval -> scheduled later -> blocked -> archived

No upload, external publishing, social token, fake view count, fake follower count, or ad metric exists.

## Safe Use

- Draft educational scripts about platform use, paper-safe learning, product truth, and safety.
- Draft caption and hashtag ideas for later review.
- Prepare thumbnail, voiceover, and scene briefs without generating raster assets.
- Score compliance risk before any external use.

## Blocked Use

- profit promises
- win-rate claims
- fake live trading or real-money routing
- fake billing activation
- fake VIP rooms or premium access
- fake certification
- fake partnership
- urgency pressure
- brand names without contract

AI Video Studio remains draft-only until future reviewed publishing systems exist.

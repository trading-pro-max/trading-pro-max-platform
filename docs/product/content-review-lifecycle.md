# Content Review Lifecycle

The content review lifecycle governs Media Office and AI Video Studio readiness.

## States

1. idea
2. draft
3. brand_review
4. guardian_review
5. legal_review
6. founder_approval
7. scheduled_later
8. blocked
9. archived

`scheduled_later` means a future planning placeholder only. It is not scheduling automation and does not publish content.

## Review Gates

- Brand review checks identity, clarity, tone, visual style, plan language, and public-safe wording.
- Guardian review checks user safety, pressure, financial advice risk, trading claims, and unsafe activation language.
- Legal review checks certifications, partnership, regulatory, billing, performance, and company-status claims.
- Founder approval is required for sensitive public use, Pro/VIP teasers, partnerships, pricing, launch language, and high-risk campaigns.

## Risk Outcomes

- safe_draft
- review_required
- founder_approval_required
- blocked

## Truth Boundary

No lifecycle state can activate external posting, social accounts, billing, broker/feed, live trading, launch, or real-money routing.

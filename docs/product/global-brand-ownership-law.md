# Global Brand Ownership Law

Status: active

Ahmad wants a global public brand that can be owned as exclusively as legally and commercially possible.

Ownership is not claimed by desire or internal naming.

Ownership requires:

- distinctiveness
- conflict review
- trademark class strategy
- domain strategy
- registration path
- legal review
- Ahmad final approval

Forbidden claims before clearance:

- globally owned
- globally exclusive
- trademark registered
- licensed
- regulated
- FINMA approved
- official Swiss company
- #1 global brand

Alkon remains private. Pro Max remains working name only.

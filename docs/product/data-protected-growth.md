# Data-Protected Growth

User data is protected before it is collected. Data growth must use minimization,
clear purpose, privacy readiness, security review, and public-safe explanations.

GPS and precise hidden tracking are blocked in this scope. No secret profiling
or silent tracking may be introduced. Future data collection must be explicit,
lawful, supportable, and reversible where possible.

Private Alkon governance data must not leak to public users.

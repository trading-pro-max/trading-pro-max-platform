# TPM Guardian And Legal Counsel

Guardian and Legal Counsel are independent protection layers.

## Guardian

Guardian protects behavior and system integrity:

- auth and accounts
- API abuse
- entitlement abuse
- feedback spam
- community safety later
- prompt injection
- bypass attempts
- suspicious automation

## Legal Counsel

Legal Counsel protects language and claims:

- product claims
- AI claims
- VIP claims
- Islamic account wording
- media wording
- risk disclosures
- launch wording
- billing and pricing wording

## Working Together

Guardian can block behavior. Legal Counsel can block wording. Both can escalate to Founder Command.

## Truth Boundary

TPM Legal Counsel is compliance guidance only. It is not a law firm, legal certification, or replacement for a qualified lawyer.

# Weather-Aware Atmosphere

Weather-aware atmosphere is readiness-only in this local laptop scope. The
product supports manual weather preference and future provider readiness, but no
provider is connected.

Weather states:
- clear: clarity and calm
- cloudy: softer contrast
- rain: focus and reduced motion
- storm: caution atmosphere only
- snow: quiet and clean
- fog: fallback or low clarity atmosphere
- heat: warm caution and reduced motion
- wind: subtle movement
- manual: user-selected atmospheric state
- unknown: safe default

Forbidden:
- GPS use
- precise tracking
- hidden tracking
- fake live weather
- weather-based trading advice
- execution decisions based on weather

# Community System

Trading Pro Max Community is a planned retention and feedback foundation. It is not an active social network in this phase.

## Planned Rooms

- Free learning space.
- Pro room.
- VIP room.
- Feedback room.
- Support room.

All rooms are planned only. No active rooms, fake members, live chat, fake moderators, fake VIP access, or social network activation exists.

## Purpose

- Learning support.
- Product feedback.
- Safe support routing.
- Future plan differentiation.
- Trust and community safety.

## Required Reviews Before Activation

- Guardian moderation.
- Legal claim review.
- Community safety policy.
- Private data protection.
- Anti-scam and anti-impersonation readiness.
- Founder approval for sensitive or paid-plan rooms.

## Forbidden Scope

- Signal rooms.
- Copy trading.
- Fake profit screenshots.
- Fake user activity.
- Fake member counts.
- Fake Pro or VIP access.
- Public Founder Command exposure.

# VIP Room Rules

VIP Rooms are planned only and must stay truthful until a future explicit activation phase.

## Required Rules

- No signal guarantees.
- No copy trading.
- No guaranteed profit.
- No win-rate claim.
- No fake VIP access.
- No fake members.
- No fake active rooms.
- No fake premium reports.
- No priority support claim until support operations exist.
- Guardian moderation required.
- Legal claim review required.
- Founder approval required before activation.

## Allowed Readiness

- Describe planned advanced coaching.
- Describe planned strategy review.
- Describe planned premium reports.
- Describe planned private rooms.
- Explain that VIP is differentiated but not active.

## Forbidden Language

- "Join active VIP room now."
- "Guaranteed signals."
- "Copy our trades."
- "Verified win-rate."
- "VIP members are earning."
- "Priority support active."

VIP value must come from education, premium review, trust, clarity, and safety, not pressure or profit promises.

# Local Readiness Law

Local maturity thresholds:
- 1 successful local day: initial local operation proof
- 3 successful local days: eligible for small internal beta planning discussion
- 7 successful local days: eligible for staging preparation discussion
- 14 successful local days: eligible for launch-readiness discussion only

None of these thresholds automatically launch Trading Pro Max.

Readiness states:
- not_started
- day_one_candidate
- local_day_passed
- repeated_local_days
- internal_beta_candidate
- staging_discussion_candidate
- launch_readiness_discussion_candidate
- blocked

Founder approval is always required, and Legal, Guardian, Product Truth, security, production, and operational gates still apply.

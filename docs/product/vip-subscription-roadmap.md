# VIP Subscription Roadmap

VIP is a future premium plan concept. This document defines the roadmap and truth boundaries. It does not activate billing, checkout, subscription state, paid access, or premium entitlement.

## VIP Value Direction

VIP should feel like a serious premium command layer, not a promise of results.

Future VIP may include:

- VIP Brain summaries
- advanced coaching
- strategy review assistant
- deeper journal analytics
- premium reports
- priority support
- private rooms
- advanced Guardian and diagnostics summaries
- early access to selected capabilities after review

## Required Before VIP Activation

- billing provider configured and validated
- entitlement model implemented and tested
- refund, cancellation, and support policy reviewed
- Legal Counsel review of every VIP claim
- Guardian review of abuse and impersonation risks
- Founder approval
- no live-money or performance guarantee language

## Blocked VIP Claims

- guaranteed profit
- higher win rate
- sure signals
- risk-free trading
- VIP users get live execution by default
- VIP users receive certified financial advice
- VIP includes Islamic/Sharia certification

## Safe VIP Language

- premium decision support
- deeper context
- advanced journal review
- priority support readiness
- future paid entitlement when configured
- no guarantee

## Current State

VIP is planned. Billing is inactive. Checkout is inactive. No user is granted fake VIP status by this roadmap.

# Founder Command Center

The Founder Command Center is the operational model behind the Founder King Command Room and Founder King Command App. It coordinates all Planet OS systems, queues, risks, and recommendations for Ahmad from one private owner-only command surface.

## Purpose

- make the Trading Pro Max planet understandable from one private surface
- show what is ready, blocked, degraded, planned, inactive, or requiring review
- convert feedback, incidents, legal issues, media drafts, and engineering tasks into action
- protect the platform from fake activation and premature launch
- keep desktop and mobile Founder workflows aligned without exposing owner controls publicly

## Command App Relationship

The Founder Command Center is the product layer. The Founder King Command App is the future private device application.

| Surface | Purpose | Current Truth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder King Command Room | private command-room concept | architecture established |
| Desktop Command App | full Founder control surface for Windows, macOS, and Linux | foundation only, no installer shipped |
| Mobile Command App | urgent review and approval surface for Android and iOS | foundation only, no app store claim |

## Operating Modes

- Observe: read-only health, readiness, and queue summaries.
- Review: inspect Guardian, Legal, Engineering, Media, Treasury, Feedback, and Ops queues.
- Approve: future explicit Founder approval for high-risk reviewed actions.
- Reject: send reviewed items back with a reason.
- Block: keep unsafe or unreviewed actions unavailable.

## Recommended Action Model

Recommendations must be explainable:

- what happened
- why it matters
- risk level
- owner role
- required review
- safe next step
- what remains blocked

No recommendation may push live trading, real-money routing, billing, public launch, social posting, broker/feed activation, production deployment, secret exposure, or legal claims without configured gates and approvals.

## No Public Route Rule

The Founder Command Center is not a public product surface. It must not appear in public navigation, SEO, normal user settings, plan pages, or Free/Pro/VIP/Enterprise account surfaces.

## Planet Earth Command Scope

The command center receives ministry reports, daily briefings, Founder approval items, Guardian alerts, Legal warnings, Engineering flags, Treasury readiness, Media queues, and citizen feedback summaries. It must not display fake users, followers, revenue, native app releases, or public launch metrics.

## Earth Hierarchy Command Scope

The command center is the receiving destination for the Earth-like chain: City -> Ministry -> State Governor -> Continent Governor -> Presidency Coordination -> Founder Command Room. It should eventually let Ahmad inspect hierarchy paths, resource categories, coordination messages, council flags, and 40-ministry readiness without exposing a public route or normal user feature.

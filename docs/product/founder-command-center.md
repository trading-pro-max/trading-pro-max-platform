# Founder Command Center

The Founder Command Center is the product concept behind the Founder King Command Room. It coordinates all ministries, centers, queues, risks, and recommendations for Ahmad.

## Purpose

- make the kingdom understandable from one private surface
- show what is ready, blocked, degraded, planned, or inactive
- convert feedback, incidents, legal issues, media drafts, and engineering tasks into action
- protect the platform from fake activation and premature launch

## Operating Modes

- Observe: read-only health, readiness, and queue summaries.
- Review: inspect details for Guardian, Legal, Engineering, Media, and Ops queues.
- Approve: future explicit Founder approval for high-risk actions.
- Block: keep unsafe or unreviewed actions unavailable.

## Recommended Action Model

Recommendations must be explainable:

- what happened
- why it matters
- risk level
- owner role
- required review
- safe next step
- what remains blocked

No recommendation may push live trading, real-money routing, billing, public launch, social posting, or legal claims without configured gates and approvals.

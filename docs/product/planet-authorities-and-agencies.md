# Planet Authorities And Agencies

Authorities and agencies sit under ministries and can review, escalate, request revision, or block unsafe action. They do not create real legal authority and do not execute public or financial actions.

| Authority | Parent Scope | Powers | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Guardian Authority | Guardian & Defense | review abuse, block unsafe bypass, escalate incidents | no invasive surveillance |
| Legal Claims Authority | Justice / Legal / Compliance | block risky claims, request safer wording | not legal advice |
| Quality Inspection Authority | Product Quality & Testing | require validation and visual acceptance | cannot fake completion |
| Rights & Brand Authority | Brand, Rights & IP | prevent copying and unsafe brand usage | cannot claim registration |
| Emergency Response Authority | Emergency & Incident | coordinate incident response | no panic automation |
| Islamic Review Desk | Special Accounts & Islamic Review | keep Islamic wording truthful | no Sharia certification claim |
| Media Review Authority | Media & Communications | classify campaign risk | no external publishing |
| Treasury Control Authority | Treasury & Economy | review monetization readiness | no billing activation |
| Companion Safety Authority | Personal Companion | bound assistant intents | no execution authority |
| AI Disclaimer Authority | AI / IQ / Brain | prevent AI overclaims | no prediction certainty |
| Anti-Scam Authority | Anti-Fraud & Anti-Manipulation | block scams and manipulation | no invasive monitoring |
| Citizen Support Authority | Citizen Support | support triage readiness | no fake support SLA |
| Engineering Review Board | Engineering & Infrastructure | validate code, APIs, and contracts | no production activation |

Every authority escalates through Founder Presidency and, where required, Founder Command.

# Swiss Precision Motion Law

Motion in Trading Pro Max must feel precise, calm, and meaningful. It is a readiness signal, not decoration.

## Motion Principles

- slow
- deliberate
- state-aware
- reduced-motion safe
- chart-safe
- non-gambling
- non-game-like
- no visual noise

## Allowed Motion

- Living Earth Mark slow orbit and subtle pulse.
- Platform Pulse compact readiness motion.
- low-intensity hover/focus transitions.
- command surfaces may use slightly stronger motion when private/internal.

## Forbidden Motion

- rapid spin
- casino glow
- flashing profit cues
- large animated overlays
- motion that competes with chart candles or execution controls
- motion that makes planned/blocked features appear active

## Reduced Motion

Every identity animation must respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. Reduced-motion mode must preserve identity meaning through static contrast, shape, and text.

## Product Truth

Motion cannot imply live execution, real money, billing, broker/feed activation, launch, paid plan activation, Swiss legal/company status, Islamic/Sharia certification, partnership, user count, revenue, or performance results.

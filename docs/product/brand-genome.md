# Brand Genome

The brand genome is immutable. Plans, states, occasions, and surfaces can layer expression on top of it, but they cannot change the core identity.

## Constants

- Earth/world signal
- Swiss-inspired precision
- trust
- clarity
- safety
- premium calm
- trading workspace
- truthful state language
- chart-first principle
- no fake claims
- no casino feeling
- no competitor copying
- no uncontracted brand use

## Boundaries

The genome never claims legal Swiss company status, certification, partnership, guaranteed profit, win-rate, paid activation, live execution, real-money routing, broker/feed activation, billing activation, or public launch.

The genome is expressed through code-native identity: SVG Earth Mark, restrained CSS motion, plan visual DNA, state labels, and brand voice. No image generation or external raster logo asset is part of the genome.

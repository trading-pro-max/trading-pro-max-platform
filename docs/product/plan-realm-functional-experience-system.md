# Plan-Realm Functional Experience System

Trading Pro Max public plans are experience realms, not labels or visual themes.

Each public realm defines visual identity, Earth perspective, feature access, TPM Assistant behavior, Journal/Coach depth, workspace behavior, plan boundaries, locked/planned/future states, reports readiness, app/platform access, support readiness, upgrade explanation, and safety truth.

## Doctrine

- Free, Pro, VIP, and Institutional are public user experience realms.
- Free is complete, not cheap. It is the active paper-safe web workspace.
- Pro and VIP are planned or entitlement-gated until real entitlement and billing gates exist.
- Institutional is future only until team/admin/audit/compliance systems exist.
- Alkon Universe is private Founder Command only and is never a public plan.
- Public copy must use Trading Pro Max, Free, Pro, VIP, Institutional, TPM Assistant, Trading Workspace, Markets, Apps / Platforms, Academy, Community, Support, Journal, Coach, Settings, Diagnostics, Readiness, Paper-safe, Planned, Future, Inactive, and Blocked.
- Public copy must not expose Alkon, Founder Command, owner controls, internal governance, construction queues, product memory internals, secrets authority, or treasury controls.

## Product Truth

This system does not activate live execution, real money, broker/feed, billing, social publishing, public launch, production secrets, or uncontrolled automation.

It must not fake Pro, VIP, or Institutional activation. It must not claim Swiss legal/company status or Islamic/Sharia certification.

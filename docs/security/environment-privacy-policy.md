# Environment Privacy Policy

Adaptive Atmosphere is privacy-safe by design.

Rules:
- No GPS.
- No precise location tracking.
- No hidden tracking.
- No exact city storage without explicit consent and reviewed future design.
- No external weather provider call in local scope.
- No external map asset.
- No weather/time/session trading advice.
- No execution, broker, billing, feed, social, or launch activation.

Allowed privacy sources:
- browser time
- timezone
- locale
- country-level or broad selected region
- future manual selected city with consent and no hidden tracking
- manual weather preference
- unavailable fallback

Diagnostics expose only public-safe environment readiness. Private Founder/Alkon
surfaces may see internal engine status, but no secrets or sensitive private
data.
